## Description

Outbound prospecting is the part of selling where nobody is waiting for you. No one raised a hand, no one filled out a form, and the person you are about to contact is in the middle of a workday that does not include you. This course is about doing that well: deciding who is genuinely worth your attention, learning enough about them to earn thirty seconds, and then using the phone and the inbox in a way that gets a specific meeting on a specific calendar.

You will work the way a real technical sales representative works — from a defined ideal customer profile, through account and buying-committee research, into live calls and written sequences, and finally into the unglamorous discipline of looking at your own pipeline and admitting which deals are real. Every technique here is something you will practice on an account list you build yourself, not something you will only read about.

By the end you will have a target list, a researched account dossier, a call script you have said out loud, a multi-touch email sequence, and a qualification scorecard you can defend to a manager. Those artifacts become the campaign you run in the final project.

## Objectives

- Define an ideal customer profile and build a qualified target list from market signals
- Use prospecting tools to research an account and its buying committee before outreach
- Open, navigate, and recover a cold call, including its predictable objections
- Write a multi-touch email sequence that asks for a specific meeting
- Qualify an outbound pipeline against a stated framework and decide what to work next
