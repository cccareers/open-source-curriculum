## Description

Somewhere in your CRM is the answer to why last quarter went the way it did. Not an opinion about it, not the story you tell yourself on the drive home — the actual sequence of opportunities you created, the stages they reached, the days they sat, and the ones that quietly stopped moving. Most reps never read it. They work hard, they make or miss quota, and the reason stays invisible because nobody ever turned the record into a number.

This course teaches you to read that record. You will start by narrowing the enormous field of sales metrics down to the handful that should actually steer a rep's week, then learn to read a pipeline report closely enough to say what it implies rather than just what it shows. From there you turn the same technique on your own funnel: find the step where deals leak, put a dollar figure on the leak, and name the most likely cause with evidence rather than a hunch. You will build a dashboard that answers a stated question instead of displaying everything the CRM knows, and you will see exactly how a week of sloppy close dates turns a red tile green.

Every lesson runs on worked numbers. You will compute win rate, average deal size, sales cycle length, pipeline coverage, and pipeline velocity by hand, from tables you can check with a calculator, and you will do it often enough that the arithmetic stops being the hard part. The course ends where analysis is supposed to end — not with a chart, but with a decision: one change, one measurable target, and a date to check whether it worked.

## Objectives

- Choose the small set of sales metrics that should drive a rep's weekly decisions
- Read a pipeline report for conversion, velocity, and coverage, and say what it implies
- Diagnose where a personal funnel is leaking and name the likely cause
- Build a CRM dashboard that answers a stated question rather than displaying everything
- Turn an analysis of sales data into a changed plan with a measurable target
