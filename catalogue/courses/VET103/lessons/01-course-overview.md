## Description

Most employers in your region have never heard that they can host a transitioning service member through DoD SkillBridge, and the few who have heard of it usually assume it is too complicated to bother with. In this course you learn to change that conversation: you become the person who can walk an employer through becoming an authorized provider and show them why it is worth the effort.

You will look at SkillBridge from the employer's side of the table. That means understanding what the provider application and agreement ask of a business, how to shape a SkillBridge placement so it leads straight into a Registered Apprenticeship, how SkillBridge hiring connects to the HIRE Vets Medallion, and how you and your board will know whether any of it produced real hires.

This is a live-virtual session built for business services staff, LVERs, and employer engagement teams. Bring one real employer from your book of business in mind; you will finish by drafting a coaching plan you could use with them next week.

## Objectives

- Explain the SkillBridge provider application and MOU requirements.
- Design a SkillBridge experience that flows directly into a Registered Apprenticeship.
- Describe HIRE Vets Medallion criteria and how SkillBridge supports them.
- Track SkillBridge-to-hire outcomes for the employer and the board.
