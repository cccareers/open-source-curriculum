## Goal

Write a coaching plan you could take into a meeting with one employer to help them become a SkillBridge provider, route participants into a Registered Apprenticeship, work toward the HIRE Vets Medallion, and track results with your board.

## The Case

Harbor Point Medical Group (fictional) is a 400-employee regional healthcare system in the Valley Regional Workforce Board area. It struggles to fill medical assistant and sterile processing technician roles. Its HR director, Lena Park, heard about SkillBridge at a chamber event and asked your AJCC for help. Harbor Point already participates in a registered medical assistant apprenticeship through a regional healthcare training partnership. It hired a few veterans last year but has no formal veteran program. A military installation is about 90 minutes away.

You may substitute a real employer from your own book of business, as long as you leave out any confidential information.

## Requirements

Your plan must include four sections:

1. **Provider application and MOU.** A completed version of the Lesson 2 coaching checklist for Harbor Point, a plain-language explanation of the post-service job-offer expectation, and a list of items to check against the current DoD SkillBridge guidance.
2. **SkillBridge-to-apprenticeship design.** Answers to the three Lesson 3 design questions and a five-phase timeline for one participant, showing the job offer and apprenticeship enrollment in place before separation.
3. **Medallion alignment.** Harbor Point's likely size category, the data you would ask HR for, and a short explanation of how the pathway supports hiring, retention, and supporting practices.
4. **Outcome tracking.** A tracker layout with employer- and participant-level fields, a sample board summary table, and the data-sharing questions you will confirm with your MIS lead.

Finish with a one-paragraph meeting agenda and next steps with dates.

## Constraints

- Do not state specific thresholds, fees, deadlines, or MOU clauses from memory. Mark them "confirm in current guidance" and name the source (DoD SkillBridge website, HIREVets.gov, your apprenticeship sponsor, your board's policy).
- Don't promise the employer apprenticeship credit for SkillBridge time or a Medallion award.
- Use only fictional or aggregate participant data.
- Keep it to two to three pages: something you would actually hand to an employer or a supervisor.

## Definition of Done

- All four sections are present and each matches one of the course objectives.
- The timeline leaves no unpaid gap between separation and employment.
- Every policy-specific detail is either from course material or flagged to confirm.
- The tracker separates employer-facing aggregate reporting from WIOA participant records.
- A colleague could pick up the plan and run the next meeting.

## Hints

- Start from the job: sterile processing may be an easier first SkillBridge placement than medical assistant, or the opposite, depending on the apprenticeship's early work process schedule.
- Bring your LVER in on the Medallion conversation; that's their home ground.
- The 90-minute distance to the installation matters for placement design. Note it.
- Reuse your Practice work from Lessons 2 to 5 rather than starting over.
