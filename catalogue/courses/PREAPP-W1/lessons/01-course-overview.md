## Description

You finished the pre-work challenge, so you already have a resume, a LinkedIn profile, and a list of employers you want to work for. Week 1 does not repeat any of that. Day one is a sending day: by the time the first lab ends you will have a live pipeline in a CRM, real messages moving out the door to regional employer partners, and a public profile that holds up when someone clicks your name.

This week is built around roughly twenty hours of live, instructor-led lab time, and these lessons are the working documents you use during and between those labs. Expect to have your CRM open in one window and the lab guide in the other. Nothing here is theory you read once — every section ends with something you do to a real record, a real message, or your real profile, and your instructor sees the result in the same session.

The week has one milestone, and it gates your pacing and your stipend: your outreach engine is running. That means a CRM with your target list staged and prioritized, at least fifty contacts initiated with personalized messages, and your first public post live. The three lessons below are simply the three parts of that engine, in the order you need them.

## Objectives

- Stand up a live prospecting pipeline in a CRM with the target list staged and prioritized
- Write personalized connection requests and follow-ups and run a daily outreach routine at quota
- Publish a skills-first LinkedIn presence and first public post

## Prerequisite Knowledge

- Completion of the five-day pre-work challenge (professional resume, LinkedIn profile, employer target list)

## Course Content

1. Course Overview
2. Pipeline & CRM Operations
3. Outreach Fundamentals
4. Personal Brand: Going Live
