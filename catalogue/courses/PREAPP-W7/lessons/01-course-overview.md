## Description

You have spent six weeks rotating through four apprenticeship pathways and building a real pipeline of outreach, conversations, and delivered work. This week you stop sampling and start choosing. By Friday you will have declared one pathway, defended that choice in writing with evidence a coach can check, and submitted a real apprenticeship application for it.

The decision is not a vote for whichever week you enjoyed most. Enthusiasm is a signal, but on its own it is not enough — you will weigh it against how you actually performed in each rotation, what your weekly reflections recorded at the time, what practitioners told you in your informational interviews, and what your coach has observed. Where those sources disagree, you will say so and explain how you resolved the conflict.

The second half of the week turns eight weeks of scattered work into something you can hand to a stranger. You will pull your pipeline data into an honest metrics story, write a Personal Prospecting Playbook that documents the process you actually use, and assemble your best artifacts from each rotation into the Outreach Portfolio capstone. That portfolio then does real work immediately: your application, your resume, and your LinkedIn profile all cite it, and every claim they make points at something in it a recruiter can open and verify.

## Objectives

- Evaluate pathway fit with evidence and justify the choice in writing
- Synthesize pipeline data into a metrics story, playbook, and portfolio of best artifacts
- Submit an apprenticeship application citing portfolio evidence, with updated resume and LinkedIn
