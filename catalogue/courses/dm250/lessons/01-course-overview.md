## Description

Social media is the channel most often run on instinct and judged on feelings. Someone picks the platforms they personally use, posts when there is time, replies when a comment stings, and reports the follower count because it is the number that is easiest to find. This course replaces every one of those habits with a decision you can defend to the person paying for it.

You will work at the depth a working social manager works at. That means choosing platforms from the audience and the objective rather than from taste, planning a calendar your team can actually sustain, adapting one idea into the formats each platform rewards, running the conversation that content produces without making a bad day worse, planning paid support on top of organic instead of instead of it, and measuring the whole thing with numbers that connect to money. Content here means planning, adaptation, and copy for social formats; the production craft of video and design belongs to the multimedia course, and the mechanics of buying media belong to the paid advertising course.

Every lesson works the same fictional business — Fernwood Coffee Roasters, a regional roaster with three cafés, a direct-to-consumer subscription, and a wholesale program — so the decisions compound instead of resetting. You will finish by planning, staffing, and measuring a full thirty-day campaign for them, and by reading a month of real-looking performance data to say what you would do next and why.

## Objectives

- Choose social platforms that fit a brand's audience and objective
- Build a content calendar with formats suited to each platform
- Manage a community and monitor brand sentiment
- Plan and target a paid social campaign that supports an organic strategy
- Measure social campaign effectiveness with platform and web analytics
