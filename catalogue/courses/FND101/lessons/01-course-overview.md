## Description

Every customer who walks through your center's front door starts a journey that ends, months or years later, in a performance report and sometimes a validation sample. Most staff only see their own slice of that journey. This course shows you the whole thing, from the first sign-in to the last follow-up contact, for Adult, Dislocated Worker, and Youth customers under WIOA Title I.

At each step you will learn who does the work, what document or record the step produces, and which rule governs it. You will also learn to tell the difference between a rule the state sets and a decision your local board has delegated to its own policy, so you know where to look when a case does not fit the usual pattern.

You will finish by tracing one fictional customer's file from start to finish, the same way a monitor or a validator would read it.

## Objectives

- Distinguish a reportable individual from a participant and identify the service that starts participation for each program.
- Sequence the 12 major steps of the participant journey and name the artifact and governing directive for each.
- Explain the 90-day exit rule and which activities do and do not extend the exit clock.
- Identify which decisions in their center are governed by state rule and which are delegated to local policy.
