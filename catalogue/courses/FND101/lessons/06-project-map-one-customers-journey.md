## Goal

Trace one fictional customer's file from the front door to validation, the way a monitor would read it. For every step, name who did the work, the artifact that proves it happened, and the rule that governs it. Then find the exit date and mark which decisions belong to the state and which belong to your local board.

## The case file

You are reviewing the file of **Carlos Reyes**, age 52, a warehouse supervisor laid off when a distribution center closed. He was served at the fictional **Valley Regional AJCC**. Here is what the file contains, in no particular order:

- Sign-in record, February 3
- CalJOBS registration, February 3
- Layoff notice from his former employer and a photo ID copy, dated February 10
- Dislocated Worker eligibility determination, February 10, with priority-of-service status recorded
- Case note, February 10: "Career counseling, discussed logistics management goals"
- Comprehensive assessment, February 17
- IEP signed February 24, goal: logistics coordinator certificate
- ITA voucher for a logistics certificate program on the ETPL, training March 9 to May 29
- Supportive service record: gas card, March 9
- Case note, May 29: "Completed program, certificate earned, copy in file"
- Case note, June 12: "Staff-assisted job search, reviewed three openings, updated resume"
- Case note, July 20: "Called to check in, no answer, left message"
- Case note, September 15: "Carlos reports hired as logistics coordinator on August 25. Follow-up."
- No other entries

## Requirements

1. **Sequence the file.** Put every item into the 12-step journey from Lesson 3. For each step, write: the role that did it, the artifact, and the governing rule or directive.
2. **Find the participation date.** State the date Carlos became a participant and the service that started it.
3. **Find the exit date.** Apply the 90-day rule from Lesson 4. List each contact after training and say whether it reset the clock.
4. **Label the decisions.** Mark at least five decisions in the file as federal, state, or local, and say where you would look to confirm each one.
5. **Flag the gaps.** List anything a validator would ask for that is missing or unclear.

## Constraints

- Use only the facts in the file. Do not invent services or dates.
- Where a rule depends on a current directive or a local policy, write "check [directive or policy name]" rather than guessing a number, limit, or code.
- Use plain language a new staff member could follow.
- Keep it to two pages, or one table plus a short note.

## Definition of Done

- All 12 steps appear in order, each with a role, artifact, and rule. A step that has no artifact in the file is marked as a gap.
- The participation date and exit date are stated, each with a one-sentence reason.
- At least five decisions are labeled by layer, with a source to check.
- At least two gaps or risks are named, with what you would do about each.
- A peer can read your map and reach the same exit date without asking you questions.

## Hints

- Participation for a Dislocated Worker starts with the first service beyond self-service or information-only, after eligibility. Look at what happened on February 10.
- Not every case note is a service. Read the July 20 note carefully.
- The September 15 note records an outcome. Ask yourself whether it is a service or follow-up.
- The supportive service amount and the ITA amount are both governed by local policy, but the ETPL listing is not.
- Step 12 has no artifact yet in this file. Think about which source documents a validator would match to the credential and the employment.
