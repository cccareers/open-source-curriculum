## Description

Most employers do not wake up wanting an apprenticeship program. They wake up with a hiring problem: a role they cannot fill, a crew that keeps turning over, or a senior worker about to retire with knowledge nobody else has. This half-day workshop teaches you to start from that problem instead of from the program brochure.

You will practice the consultative conversation that business services staff actually have with employers: asking good questions, putting real numbers in front of a decision-maker, answering the worries about cost and paperwork without getting defensive, and recommending the model that fits the employer in front of you. Apprenticeship is the main tool, but you will also learn when on-the-job training, incumbent worker training, or SkillBridge is the better first step.

The session closes with a role-played employer meeting where you put the whole conversation together and leave with a clear next step agreed.

## Objectives

- Run a discovery conversation that surfaces an employer's talent pain.
- Present a business case for apprenticeship using wage, retention, and subsidy numbers.
- Respond to the five most common employer objections.
- Choose between starting a new program, joining an intermediary program, or using OJT.
