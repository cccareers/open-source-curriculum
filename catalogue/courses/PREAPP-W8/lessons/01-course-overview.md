## Description

This is the week the network you built shows up in a room. For seven weeks you have been adding contacts, running outreach, sitting in informational interviews, and building a capstone. Week 8 asks the only question that was ever really on the table: can you stand in front of the people who could hire you and make them believe what you did?

The week is roughly twenty hours of live lab, and it runs in three moves. First you build and rehearse a seven-to-ten minute results presentation and put it through structured peer feedback — specific and actionable, not "that was great." Then you deliver it at Demo Day to CCC staff, regional employer partners, and the network contacts you invited yourself. Finally you plan the landing: a thirty-day transition plan into apprenticeship entry, and a hard verification that your CRM data, your LinkedIn connections, and your published content are actually yours and travel with you when the program ends.

Two things end this week. Your Demo Day presentation is delivered, and your transition plan is on file. Bring your capstone, your pipeline numbers, and your portfolio to every lab — every session in Week 8 works on the real artifact you are going to present, not a practice version of it.

## Objectives

- Build and rehearse a 7-10 minute results presentation with structured peer feedback
- Present the capstone to staff, employer partners, and network contacts at Demo Day
- Complete a 30-day transition plan and verify ownership of the network and data built
