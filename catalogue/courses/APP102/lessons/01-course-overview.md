## Description

You already know how to write a standard OJT contract for a single new hire who will be proficient in a few months. Registered Apprenticeship breaks that template. Apprenticeships run for years, apprentices move up a wage schedule, employers often hire several apprentices at once, and the classroom portion of the program may be paid from a different WIOA funding stream. California has no state OJT directive, and local OJT templates are rarely built for this.

In this hands-on workshop you will write an OJT contract and a cohort training plan that actually fit an apprenticeship. You will learn to justify the reimbursement rate you choose, scale training hours to the occupation and the apprenticeship's work process schedule, combine OJT and ITA funds for the same apprentice without paying for anything twice, and set up the records, invoices, and monitoring visits that let you close the contract cleanly.

Because board OJT policies vary widely, you will bring your own board's policy into every exercise and treat it as a variable you fill in. You will leave with a completed contract package for a sample apprenticeship that you can adapt on Monday.

## Objectives

- Determine the appropriate reimbursement rate (50, 75, or 90 percent) and document the justifying factors under 20 CFR 680.730 and WSD25-03.
- Build a cohort training plan that covers multiple apprentices under one contract.
- Set training duration and hours using occupation complexity and the apprenticeship's work process schedule.
- Combine OJT and ITA funding for one apprentice without overlap.
- Specify the employer records, invoices, and monitoring visits required to close the contract cleanly.

## Prerequisite Knowledge

- APP 101 (Registered Apprenticeship for Workforce Professionals)
- Working knowledge of WIOA Title I eligibility (FND 102 or equivalent)
