## Description

An apprenticeship is paid for by several hands at once: the employer pays wages, a college or adult school delivers related instruction, and WIOA, state apprenticeship grants, and veterans' benefits can each cover a different slice. When you cannot say who pays for which piece, money gets left on the table or, worse, the same cost gets billed twice.

In this live-virtual session you will sort each part of an apprenticeship into the funding streams that can legally pay for it, learn the rules that keep one cost from being claimed by two sources, run the numbers on a sample apprentice, and see how a veteran's GI Bill benefits fit alongside everything else.

You will leave with a one-page braiding worksheet you can use with your next apprentice or employer partner. Keep your own board's local policies handy; several decisions in this course depend on them.

## Objectives

- Match each apprenticeship component (OJT, related instruction, supportive services, program development) to its eligible funding sources.
- Apply the no-double-dip rules, such as RSI hours not claimable when funded by a CAI grant.
- Calculate an AIF-supported funding scenario for a sample apprentice.
- Identify when GI Bill OJT and apprenticeship benefits apply and how the housing allowance steps down.
