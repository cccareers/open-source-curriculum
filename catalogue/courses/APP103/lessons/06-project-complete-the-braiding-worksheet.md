## Goal

Complete a one-page braiding worksheet for one sample apprentice that assigns every cost component to an eligible funding source, with no cost paid twice, and shows the sponsor's net cost after AIF and WIOA.

## The case

Tamika, 31, was laid off from a warehouse supervisor job and is enrolled at the Valley Regional AJCC as a WIOA Dislocated Worker. She has been accepted into year one of a logistics coordinator apprenticeship sponsored by Harbor Freightways, a DAS-registered program that Harbor confirms is AIF-eligible.

- Wages: $22/hour, 2,000 hours in year one.
- Related instruction: 180 hours at Harbor's partner community college. The college received a CAI grant to launch the program; the grant covers the first 60 instruction hours for the opening group, which includes Tamika. The college plans to claim RSI for instruction.
- Textbooks and an industry certification exam: $1,100 total, not covered by the college.
- Supportive services needs: safety boots, a laptop for online coursework, and a monthly transit pass, $1,000 total.
- Harbor's mentor and coordination costs for Tamika: $2,800.
- Workshop figures (fictional, not current rates): AIF of $3,000 per eligible apprentice, which Harbor will apply to mentor costs first and any remainder to Tamika's boots; Valley Regional's OJT policy reimburses 50% of wages for the first 1,000 hours.

## Requirements

Your worksheet must fit on one page and include:

1. A row for each cost item, grouped under the four components: OJT, related instruction, supportive services, program development and program costs.
2. For each row: the cost, the single source that pays it, and the basis (OJT contract, RSI claim, CAI grant, AIF, ITA or training cost, supportive services, employer).
3. A clear split of the 180 instruction hours between the CAI grant and RSI.
4. The AIF calculation in steps, including where any AIF remainder goes.
5. A net-cost line for Harbor Freightways.
6. A short funding-confirmation case note (four to six lines) recording who confirmed each source.

## Constraints

- One payer per cost. No instruction hour may be claimed both as CAI-funded and as RSI.
- Apply sources in this order: already funded at the source (employer, RSI, grant), then AIF, then WIOA as the gap-filler.
- Use only the workshop figures given. Label every rate as a workshop figure and mark where your board's policy or current DAS guidance would replace it.
- Do not use real participant or employer data.

## Definition of done

- Every cost item from the case appears exactly once.
- The CAI and RSI hours add up to 180 with no overlap.
- AIF and WIOA amounts add up correctly, and the net cost equals total costs minus all outside funding.
- A colleague could read your case note and know which source pays for what, and how you confirmed it.
- You can explain your choices in under two minutes in the session's breakout room.

## Hints

- Start with the instruction hours; the CAI-versus-RSI split is the easiest place to double-dip.
- If AIF covers the full mentor cost, check how much remains and whether that changes what WIOA pays for boots.
- Revisit the table in lesson 2 if you are unsure which stream can pay for a component.
- Tamika is not a veteran, so the GI Bill does not appear. Add a margin note on what would change if she were.
