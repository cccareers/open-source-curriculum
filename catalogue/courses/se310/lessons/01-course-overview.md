## Description

Signing a contract is the middle of the story, not the end of it. In subscription software the money that keeps a company alive is the money that comes back next year, and the person the customer met during the sale is usually the person they judge the whole company by afterwards. That is you. This course is about the year that starts the day the deal closes.

You will work through the account lifecycle in the order it actually happens: handing a new customer to the people who will implement for them without dropping anything on the floor, sitting in account reviews and hearing the problem the customer has not said out loud yet, spotting the accounts that are quietly drifting toward cancellation while there is still time to change the outcome, and finding the growth that is already sitting inside a healthy account. Underneath all of it runs a habit that feels clerical and is not: keeping your records honest, so that what your company believes about its customers matches what is true.

The work here is conversational. Other courses in this pathway build the economics of retention and the mechanics of the CRM; this one is where you open your mouth in front of a customer and where you type what you heard into a system somebody else will forecast from. Every technique is practiced on a real-feeling account, and the course ends with you writing a complete retention plan for one.

## Objectives

- Plan an account's first 90 days so the customer reaches value before the first renewal conversation
- Use active listening in account reviews to surface risk the customer has not stated
- Detect churn risk early and run a renewal conversation from evidence
- Identify and qualify an upsell or cross-sell inside an existing account
- Maintain account and opportunity records so the retention picture stays accurate

- Advanced SaaS Sales Strategies (se201), for retention and expansion metrics
