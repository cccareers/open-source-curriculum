## Description

Registered Apprenticeship is one of the strongest career outcomes you can offer a job seeker, but many of the people you serve are not ready to compete for an apprenticeship slot on day one. Youth with little work history, adults returning from incarceration, and job seekers missing a math credential or a driver's license often need a structured step before they apply. Pre-apprenticeship is that step, when it is built well and connected to a real program.

In this live-virtual session you will learn how to tell a quality pre-apprenticeship from a generic job-readiness class, how to pay for it with the WIOA funds you already manage, how youth pre-apprenticeship can count toward your work experience spending requirement, and how to make sure participants actually land in an apprenticeship when they finish.

You will work through one running case from start to finish and leave with a draft pre-apprenticeship plan you can adapt for a real participant on your caseload. Bring your board's local policies on ITAs, youth work experience, and supportive services if you have them; you will be asked to fill in local variables as you go.

## Objectives

- Identify the elements of a quality pre-apprenticeship with a direct link to a registered program.
- Fund pre-apprenticeship through WIOA Adult, Dislocated Worker, or Youth.
- Count youth pre-apprenticeship toward work experience requirements.
- Design a warm handoff from pre-apprenticeship to apprenticeship.
