## Start with the person, then the program

You do not "fund a pre-apprenticeship." You fund a **participant** in a pre-apprenticeship, using a WIOA Title I program that participant is eligible for. So every funding decision starts with two questions, in this order:

1. **Which Title I program is this person enrolled in, or eligible for?** Adult, Dislocated Worker, or Youth.
2. **Which funding mechanism fits this program and this training?** Most often an Individual Training Account (ITA), but not always.

Get the first question wrong and the rest of the file falls apart at monitoring.

## Path 1: Adult or Dislocated Worker with an ITA

For Adult and Dislocated Worker participants, training is normally paid through an ITA, and an ITA can only be issued for a program on the **Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL)**. That makes the ETPL check your first practical step.

| Check | Where to look | If the answer is no |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the pre-apprenticeship on the ETPL? | California's ETPL (CalJOBS) program listing | Ask the provider whether they plan to apply; look at other paths below |
| Is the listed program the same one the participant will attend? | Compare program name, length, cost, and location | Do not issue the ITA against a different listing |
| Does the cost fit your board's ITA limits? | Your board's local ITA policy | Braid with other funds or adjust the plan |
| Has the participant received career services and an assessment showing training is appropriate? | The participant's IEP/ISS and case notes | Complete that first; training eligibility must be documented |

**Worked example.** Denise, 34, was laid off from a warehouse job and is enrolled as a Dislocated Worker. She wants to enter a carpentry apprenticeship but needs to build tool skills and pass the sponsor's entry test. Valley Build Pathways is on the ETPL. Her case manager documents the assessment, confirms the ETPL listing matches the program Denise will attend, checks the cost against the board's ITA cap, and issues an ITA. Supportive services (bus passes, work boots) are authorized separately under the board's supportive services policy.

## Path 2: Youth

Youth funding works differently. Pre-apprenticeship fits naturally into the youth program because work experience, including pre-apprenticeship, is one of the WIOA youth program elements. For a youth participant you have more than one way to pay:

- **As a work experience activity** paid from youth funds, through a contract or agreement with the provider. This is the path that can count toward the youth work experience requirement, covered in the next lesson.
- **As occupational skills training through an ITA**, where your board's youth policy allows ITAs for eligible youth (generally older out-of-school youth). Check your board's youth ITA policy for the age and eligibility rules it applies.
- **Through co-enrollment.** A youth aged 18 or older may also be eligible for the Adult program. Co-enrollment lets you braid an Adult ITA for training with youth-funded services such as mentoring and follow-up. Follow your board's co-enrollment policy and coding rules.

## Stipends and supportive services

Pre-apprenticeship participants often drop out for money reasons: they cannot afford ten weeks without income. Two tools help:

- **Supportive services** (transportation, tools, gear, childcare) under your board's supportive services policy. These are separate from the training cost.
- **Stipends.** The course catalog identifies **WSD23-08** as the state guidance governing stipends in this context. Before you promise a participant any stipend, read the current version of WSD23-08 and your board's local policy for who can receive one, what it can be tied to (such as attendance or completion), how it is documented, and how it is paid. Do not quote a stipend amount to a participant until you have confirmed it.

## Decision guide

```text
1. Enrolled/eligible program?  Adult | Dislocated Worker | Youth | co-enrolled
2. Adult or DW?
     -> Pre-apprenticeship on ETPL? yes -> ITA (within local cap)
                                    no  -> ask provider about ETPL; consider other funds
3. Youth?
     -> Pay as work experience (contract/agreement)?  -> counts toward WEX requirement
     -> Or ITA under board youth policy?              -> check eligibility rules
     -> Age 18+? consider Adult co-enrollment and braiding
4. Barriers to completion? -> supportive services; check WSD23-08 for stipends
5. Document: eligibility, assessment, ETPL match, funding source, cost, services
```
