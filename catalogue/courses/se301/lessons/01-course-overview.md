## Description

Every conversation you have with a buyer disappears the moment it ends unless something writes it down. The CRM is that something. It is where a name becomes a lead, a lead becomes an opportunity, an opportunity becomes a number your manager commits to, and the whole thing becomes a record somebody can audit six months later. Reps who treat it as administrative overhead end up with a pipeline nobody believes, including themselves. Reps who operate it well get their week planned for them, their follow-up handled automatically, and a forecast they can defend in a room full of skeptics.

This course teaches CRM operation as a transferable skill rather than a vendor certification. You will start with the data model — the handful of objects and relationships that every one of these systems implements under slightly different names — because once you can see that model, the three platforms this course works through stop looking like three unrelated tools. Salesforce, HubSpot, and GoHighLevel each get a full session, and each session is really the same question asked again: where do people live here, where does the sale live, how does work get recorded, and what does this system call the things you already understand.

The back half is about making the system work for you instead of the other way round. You will design automation that removes manual steps and enforces a qualification framework, learn the hygiene rules that keep pipeline data trustworthy enough to be worth automating, and finish by turning opportunity records into a forecast with a stated method and stated assumptions. The final project asks you to do all of it in one place: configure a pipeline, work it, clean it, and forecast it.

## Objectives

- Explain how a CRM models a sale — objects, records, stages, and their relationships
- Work leads and opportunities through a Salesforce pipeline correctly
- Manage contacts, deals, and activity in HubSpot
- Operate pipelines and campaign automation in GoHighLevel
- Design an automation or workflow that removes manual steps from a sales process
- Apply CRM hygiene rules that keep pipeline data trustworthy
- Produce a defensible forecast from CRM opportunity data

## Prerequisite Knowledge

- Mastering Outbound Prospecting (se202), for the lead flow this course records and automates
