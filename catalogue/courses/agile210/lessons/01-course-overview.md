## Description

Everything you have learned in this pathway converges here. You have written prompts and judged their output, wired automations across tools, moved data in and out of databases and APIs, prototyped against real users, and reviewed your own work for security, privacy, and fairness. This course asks you to do all of it at once, on one problem, for one client, and to finish.

The shape of the work is deliberate. You will spend the first stage not building at all, but scoping: naming a real client problem, agreeing what "solved" means, and writing a brief with success criteria somebody else could measure. Then you design, then you build the data and integration layer, then the AI workflow on top of it, then you review the whole thing for risk and fix what the review finds, then you test it until it survives being used badly, and finally you demonstrate it, hand it over, and write it up. Each stage ends at an instructor checkpoint, and each stage inherits the artifacts of the one before it. There is one capstone, built in seven passes, not seven small projects. Throughout the course, each stage is referred to by its lesson number: "stage 02" is scoping (this course's lesson 2), "stage 05" is the AI workflow, and "stage 08" is the demo and handover.

No new tools and no new concepts are introduced. Every technique this course asks for was taught earlier in the pathway, and the challenge is integration rather than novelty: holding a design steady while the details change, keeping a build honest when the deadline is close, and being able to explain to a non-technical person exactly what you built, what it costs, and where it can be wrong. Budget the forty hours as build time, protect the scoping stage from being rushed, and expect the finished artifact to be the strongest single item in your portfolio.

## Objectives

- Scope a capstone around a real client problem and write a solution brief with measurable success criteria
- Design the solution and plan the prototype, showing how each component meets a stated requirement
- Build the capstone's data and integration layer, connecting the sources and stores the solution depends on
- Build the capstone's AI workflow, with prompts, chaining, and automation logic that meet the brief
- Review the capstone for security, privacy, and responsible-AI risk and remediate what the review finds
- Test, troubleshoot, and harden the capstone until it survives realistic use
- Demonstrate the capstone, hand it over with documentation, and write it up for a portfolio

## Prerequisite Knowledge
