## Description

This week is the AI Prompt Engineering and Automation rotation, and the rule for the whole week is that nothing stays theoretical. Every skill you learn in the morning gets pointed at your own live outreach pipeline the same day. You are not going to practice on made-up companies or sample contacts; you are going to research the accounts you are actually working, draft the messages you are actually sending, and automate the steps you are actually repeating.

You will start with structured prompting — learning to give a model a role, context, a task, and an output format instead of a vague one-line ask — and you will run the same prompt through both Claude and ChatGPT so you can see with your own eyes how two strong models answer the same question differently. From there you will judge those answers, and the standard you will be held to is verifiability, not fluency: a confident paragraph you cannot check is worth less than a plain one you can. Then you will turn that skill on your pipeline, using AI to accelerate prospect research and message personalization while critiquing every draft for fabricated details, generic filler, and phrasing that does not sound like you.

The week closes with your project. You will find one repetitive step in your own workflow, design an automation for it, build it, and demo it to the cohort with a defensible estimate of the time it saves. That working automation and its time-saved number are this week's milestone, and both of them have to survive questions from the room.

## Objectives

- Prompt Claude and ChatGPT with structure, comparing outputs and judging fitness for purpose
- Use AI to accelerate research and personalization while critiquing drafts for accuracy and authenticity
- Design and build a working automation with a measurable time-saved estimate
