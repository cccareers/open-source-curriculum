## Goal

Build a one-page Regional Apprenticeship Fact Sheet that a coworker could hand to an employer or job seeker tomorrow. It should explain who does what in apprenticeship, list real new-collar programs in your region, and explain the ETPL link correctly.

**Scenario.** You work at the fictional *Valley Regional AJCC*. Your manager, Dana, has noticed that front-desk and business services staff give different answers about apprenticeship. She asks you to draft a fact sheet everyone can use. If you would rather use your own region, do so. Otherwise, use your Lesson 4 scan as your program list.

## Requirements

Your fact sheet must include these sections:

1. **What Registered Apprenticeship is.** Two or three plain sentences, including how it differs from pre-apprenticeship, OJT and internships.
2. **Who does what.** A short table naming the role of the Office of Apprenticeship, DAS, the sponsor, the intermediary and the related instruction provider. Add one line on where the AJCC fits.
3. **New-collar programs near you.** At least three programs, at least two outside the building trades. For each, list the occupation, sector, sponsor or intermediary, registration status, whether it is hiring, and a contact or next step.
4. **Apprenticeship and the ETPL.** Two or three sentences explaining why registered programs are automatically eligible for the ETPL under WSD25-02, and what a sponsor still has to do to be listed.
5. **Who to call.** Your office's contact for apprenticeship questions, plus where to confirm registration (DAS).

## Constraints

- One page, plain language, written for an employer or job seeker, not for staff.
- No dollar amounts, deadlines or reimbursement rates unless you have confirmed them in your board's current policy. Mark any you include with the source.
- Every program listing shows its source and the date you checked it.
- Use no real participant information.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] All five sections are present.
- [ ] The roles table correctly separates OA (federal) from DAS (California's registration agency), and does not say the AJCC or board registers programs.
- [ ] At least three regional programs are listed with status, source and date, two or more of them new-collar.
- [ ] The ETPL section explains *why* (registration is the quality review) and not just *that*.
- [ ] A coworker could read it in under three minutes and answer, "Who do I call to register a program?"

## Hints

- Start from your Lesson 3 practice table and your Lesson 4 scan; most of the content already exists.
- If a program's status is unclear, say "Confirm with sponsor" rather than guessing.
- Read your ETPL paragraph aloud. If it sounds like a promise of funding, rewrite it.
- Ask one coworker to review it and tell you which sentence confused them most.
