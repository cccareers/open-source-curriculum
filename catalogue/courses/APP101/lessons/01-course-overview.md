## Description

Employers and job seekers ask you about apprenticeship more every year, and the word gets used loosely. This course gives you a plain-language foundation in how Registered Apprenticeship actually works in California and nationally, so you can answer those questions accurately and know who to call next.

You will learn to tell a registered program apart from look-alike models, sort out which agency does what, find the new-collar programs already running in your own region, and explain why registered programs have a straightforward path onto California's Eligible Training Provider List. The focus is on occupations outside the traditional building trades, including IT, healthcare, education, and advanced manufacturing.

You will finish by building a short regional fact sheet you can use with customers and coworkers the following week.

## Objectives

- Explain the difference between Registered Apprenticeship, pre-apprenticeship, OJT, and internships.
- Describe the roles of the Office of Apprenticeship, DAS, sponsors, intermediaries, and related instruction providers.
- Identify new-collar apprenticeship occupations active in their region.
- Explain why every registered program is automatically eligible for the ETPL under WSD25-02.
