## Description

Paid advertising is the fastest way to put a message in front of someone who is already looking for what you sell, and it is the fastest way to spend money on people who are not. Every click costs whether or not it turns into anything, so the difference between a profitable account and an expensive one is rarely the size of the budget. It is the structure, the keywords, the copy, and the measurement underneath them.

This course puts you in the operator's seat. You will work primarily in Google Ads, with paid social on Meta and LinkedIn as the second platform family, and you will build almost everything against one running example: a residential heating and cooling contractor competing for expensive clicks in a local market. You will do the arithmetic yourself, by hand, because judging a campaign is a numbers job before it is a creative one. Cost per click, cost per acquisition, return on ad spend, and contribution are the vocabulary of this trade, and you will be fluent in them by the end.

You will work in sandbox and draft campaigns throughout, so nothing here requires you to spend live budget. What it does require is that you can defend every choice you make: why that match type, why that bidding strategy, why you called an A/B test finished when you did, and why the change you are proposing is likely to earn money rather than simply look like work.

## Objectives

- Explain how a paid search auction decides which ad shows and what it costs
- Structure a paid account into campaigns and ad groups that can be optimized
- Select keywords and match types, and use negatives to protect budget
- Write compliant ad copy and assets that match the query and the landing page
- Set budgets, bidding strategies, and targeting for a stated campaign goal
- Plan a paid social campaign and choose the audiences for it
- Implement conversion tracking and verify that it reports correctly
- Improve the conversion rate of a landing page receiving paid traffic
- Design a valid A/B test on ads or landing pages and interpret the result
- Judge campaign performance on return on investment and decide what to change
