## Why local knowledge beats general knowledge

Knowing how SkillBridge works is half the job. The other half is knowing **where it happens near you**: which military installations send transitioning service members into your area, and which employers in your region already host SkillBridge participants. When Andre, our running case, walks in, "SkillBridge is a DoD program" helps a little. "Coastline Freight hosts participants out of the installation's transition office, and we have a business services contact there" helps a lot.

The catalog notes that the workforce system is largely absent from SkillBridge. Building a short regional reference sheet is one of the simplest ways your center can change that.

## Two things to map

**1. Installations.** Service members in your region may be stationed at a nearby base, or they may be relocating to your area from somewhere else entirely. List:

- installations within or near your local area,
- each installation's **transition office** (the office that runs the Transition Assistance Program and usually advises on SkillBridge), and
- the name or role of a contact there, if your center has one.

**2. Providers.** Authorized SkillBridge providers and their programs are listed on the official DoD SkillBridge website, which has a searchable list and map of opportunities by location. Search for your counties and nearby cities and note:

- the provider's name and industry,
- the occupation the program trains for,
- whether the program is in person in your area, and
- whether your center already has a relationship with that employer (check with your business services team).

Listings change. Treat the website as the current source and your sheet as a snapshot with a date on it.

## A worked example

Valley Regional Workforce Board is a fictional board serving three inland counties. A case manager spends 45 minutes building the following sheet.

| Type | Name (fictional) | Detail | Our connection | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Installation | Mesa Ridge Air Station | Transition office on base, about 40 minutes from the north AJCC | None yet; JVSG staff to call | [date] |
| Provider | Coastline Freight Systems | Warehouse operations supervisor, in person, two county sites | Existing OJT employer | [date] |
| Provider | Sierra Line Electric | Lineworker pre-apprenticeship, in person | Registered apprenticeship partner | [date] |
| Provider | BrightPath Health IT | Help desk technician, remote | None | [date] |

Notice what the sheet already tells the team:

- Two providers are employers the board **already works with**. Business services can mention to them that the center sees transitioning service members, without needing to recruit them into SkillBridge.
- One provider is remote. Participants in that program may live in your area without any local employer connection.
- The nearest installation has no contact yet. That is the first relationship to build, and JVSG staff are often the natural lead.

## Who keeps the sheet current

Decide this as a team, not alone. Common patterns:

- The JVSG staff member owns installation contacts.
- Business services owns provider entries.
- A front-desk lead keeps a printed copy at intake and asks for a refresh on a set schedule.

Your board's local policy, or your center manager, decides who owns partner lists and where they are stored. Do not store participant names or personal information on this sheet; it is about places and programs, not people.

## Practice

**Exercise: Build your region's sheet.** Using the official DoD SkillBridge website and what your center already knows:

1. List every installation within reasonable commuting distance of your AJCC, plus its transition office.
2. Find at least three SkillBridge providers with programs in or near your local area.
3. For each provider, check with your business services team and fill in "Our connection."
4. Date the sheet and identify who will refresh it next.

**Discussion prompt (live-virtual):** Share one surprise from your search. Was there a provider you already work with that you did not know hosted SkillBridge participants?
