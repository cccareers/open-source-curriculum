## Description

Every year, service members leave the military with months of planning behind them, and some of them spend their final stretch of service training inside a civilian employer while still on military pay. That program is DoD SkillBridge, and most AJCC staff have never been trained on it. When one of these people walks into your center, you want to know what they are in the middle of and what they need from you.

In this short course you will learn how SkillBridge works, what employers who host participants have promised, where the program shows up in your own region, and how to spot a SkillBridge participant at the front desk and open the right conversation. You will follow one fictional service member through the course so each piece connects to a real moment at your center.

This course is written for all frontline staff and JVSG staff. It is training, not policy: wherever a rule depends on current DoD or Service guidance or on your board's local policy, the lessons tell you where to check rather than giving you a number to memorize.

## Objectives

- Explain SkillBridge eligibility, duration, and command-approval requirements.
- Describe provider MOU obligations, including the post-service job-offer requirement.
- Identify local SkillBridge providers and installations in their region.
- Recognize a SkillBridge participant at the front desk and start the right conversation.
