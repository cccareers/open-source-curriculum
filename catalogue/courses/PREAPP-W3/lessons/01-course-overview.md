## Description

For two weeks you have been doing the work: researching companies, writing outreach, booking conversations, and keeping a pipeline. This week you meet the profession that does that work full time and gets paid for it. Technical sales is the first of your pathway rotations, and it is the one closest to the skill you already have.

The week is built around live practice, not lecture. You will map the roles on a real sales team and the numbers each one is measured on, then run a recorded mock discovery call with an industry practitioner playing the buyer. You will score your own pipeline against explicit qualification criteria, rehearse responses to the three objections you will hear most often, and finish by treating your personal pipeline as a funnel — computing stage-to-stage conversion, finding where prospects stall, and presenting a corrective plan.

By Friday you should be able to say whether this profession fits you, and back that up with evidence: a recorded call you can play back, a scored pipeline, and a funnel review you delivered out loud.

## Objectives

- Distinguish SDR/BDR, AE, and sales engineering roles and how sales teams are measured
- Apply a discovery framework in a live mock call and score the pipeline against qualification criteria
- Handle objections and analyze the personal pipeline as a funnel
