## Description

Your first month in an America's Job Center of California (AJCC) can feel like walking into a conversation that started years ago. People use acronyms you have never heard, refer to policies you cannot find, and make decisions you are not sure you are allowed to make yet. This course gives you a structured way through that month, so you are not left to learn only from whoever happens to sit next to you.

You will build a short onboarding record as you go: a map of who does what in your local system, a local policy you have tracked back to the state directive behind it, your CalJOBS access steps, and a shadowing checklist you can use with experienced staff. Each module is self-paced and takes about an hour, and each one ends with a practical exercise you complete using your own center's information.

By the end, you should know where you fit, where to look things up, how to protect participant information, and when a decision belongs to your supervisor instead of you.

## Objectives

- Describe the roles of the Local Board, AJCC Operator, Career Services Provider, EDD, and partners.
- Locate their board's local policies and the state directives behind them.
- Complete the CalJOBS access requirements and explain why they matter.
- Follow a structured shadowing checklist and know which decisions require a supervisor.
