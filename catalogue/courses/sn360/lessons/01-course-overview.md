## Description

Everything you have configured so far in this pathway has served people inside the company. Customer Service Management points the same platform outward. The person filing a case is now an external customer with a contract, an entitlement, a support level someone sold them, and no patience for being told to open a ticket in a tool they cannot see. That change of audience is what makes CSM its own implementation discipline rather than a rename of incident management.

You will build the customer picture first, because a case that is not attached to the right account and contact cannot be routed, entitled, measured, or trusted. From there you will configure the case lifecycle, then the entitlements and contracts that decide whether a case is supported at all and what response commitment it carries. The second half of the course is the customer-facing surface: the portal your customers log into, the knowledge they read instead of contacting you, the Virtual Agent topic that resolves a request without an agent, and the routing engine that puts the cases that survive all of that in front of the right person.

The course closes with integration and measurement, because a CSM instance almost never stands alone. You will connect it to a CRM or support tool and make the failures visible instead of silent, then automate the collection of the handful of numbers a service manager actually asks for. The final project asks you to assemble those pieces into one working implementation for a fictional customer, which is exactly the shape of the work you will be handed on a real engagement.

## Objectives

- Model accounts, contacts, consumers, and installed products so cases attach to the right customer
- Configure case types, states, and the case lifecycle so support work follows a defined process
- Configure entitlements and service contracts that drive customer-facing SLAs
- Customize the customer service portal so external customers can serve themselves securely
- Publish customer-facing knowledge with the access controls external users require
- Build a Virtual Agent topic that resolves a common customer request without a human agent
- Automate case routing and assignment using matching rules, queues, and agent capacity
- Integrate CSM with a CRM or support tool and handle synchronization failures safely
- Automate the collection of customer service KPIs and present them on a dashboard
