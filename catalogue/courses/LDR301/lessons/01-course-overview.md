## Description

Every AJCC runs on a process, but very little of it is written down. Ask five people how a customer gets from the front desk to follow-up and you will hear five versions, each one missing the steps that only one colleague knows how to do. When that colleague is out sick, leaves, or retires, the step goes with them.

In this full-day working session you and your team will map your own center's process as swim lanes, marking who does each step, what record it produces, and which rule governs it. You will not start from a blank wall: you begin from CCC's statewide base map and adjust it to match how your center really works.

By the end of the day you will know where your process depends on memory instead of procedure, which gaps to fix first, and how to keep the map accurate as policies and systems change. You leave with a draft map, a short list of procedures to write, and a routine for keeping both alive.

## Objectives

- Map their center's process from sign-in to follow-up in swim-lane form.
- Identify undocumented steps, duplicate data entry, and handoff failures.
- Prioritize which procedures to write down first.
- Maintain the map as policies and systems change, including the CalJOBS modernization.
