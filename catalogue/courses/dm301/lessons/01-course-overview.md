## Description

Marketing without measurement is a series of confident guesses. This course hands you the instruments to check them: a Google Analytics 4 property you configure and verify yourself, Google Search Console working alongside it, and the qualitative tools — usability testing, heatmaps, session recordings, and direct user feedback — that explain what the numbers alone never will.

You will work the whole arc on one fictional online retailer, against one set of figures that everything has to reconcile against. That is how you learn the part most analytics training skips: how to tell a real change from ordinary week-to-week variation, why a twelve percent move on two hundred sessions is noise, and how much traffic a business actually needs before a conversion test can settle anything at all.

By the end you should be able to open a report, work out what is true in it, say plainly what it cannot tell you, and turn one finding into a change somebody can act on — along with a way to know afterwards whether it worked.

## Objectives

- Set up a GA4 property and verify that it collects the events a business cares about
- Choose KPIs for a marketing goal and read behavior reports against them
- Combine Search Console and analytics data to answer a measurement question
- Turn an analytics finding into a specific marketing decision
- Run a usability test and report what it reveals
- Use heatmaps, session recordings, and user feedback to locate experience problems
- Propose and justify a change intended to lift conversion rate
