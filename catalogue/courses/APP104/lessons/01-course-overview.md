## Description

An apprentice can finish a four-year program, earn a journey-level certificate, and raise their wages every year along the way, and none of it shows up in your board's performance if the participant was never enrolled, was enrolled in the wrong program, or was coded as something other than an apprentice. This course is about making that work visible: deciding when co-enrollment in WIOA Title I makes sense, recording the right services and flags in CalJOBS, and capturing the outcomes an apprenticeship produces.

You will work one running case from intake to follow-up, the way you would on the job. Along the way you will practice the decisions case managers, MIS administrators, and data staff make together, and you will learn where to look when a code or rule needs to be confirmed against current state and federal guidance.

Bring your board's local policies on co-enrollment, training, and follow-up if you have them. Several decisions in this course depend on local choices, and you will get the most out of the exercises by filling those in for your own area.

## Objectives

- Identify when to co-enroll an apprentice in WIOA Title I and which program fits.
- Select the correct activity codes and PIRL flags for apprenticeship-related services.
- Record credential attainment and Measurable Skill Gains for apprentices.
- Plan exit and follow-up timing for a participant in a multi-year program.

## Prerequisite Knowledge

- FND 101 (The Participant Journey) or equivalent
