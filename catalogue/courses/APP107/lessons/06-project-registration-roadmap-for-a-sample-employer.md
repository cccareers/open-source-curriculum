## Goal

Produce a one-to-two page registration roadmap for a fictional employer, the kind you could send after a first meeting. It should cover the documents, the advisory body, a realistic timeline, and a register-or-join recommendation.

### The case

**Mesa Valley Medical Group** is a 300-employee outpatient health system served by Valley Regional Workforce Board. Its HR director, Lena Park, tells your business services team:

- They want to train 8 current front-desk workers into **medical assistant** roles over the next year, then hire about 6 more each year.
- They have job descriptions for medical assistants and a current pay scale, but no wage progression for trainees.
- A local community college offers a medical assistant certificate, but no one has asked whether it could serve as RSI.
- Lena has heard that a regional healthcare intermediary "does apprenticeships" but has not contacted it.
- Leadership wants apprentices started "this fall," about four months away.
- Two of the front-desk workers are current WIOA participants at your AJCC.

## Requirements

Your roadmap must include:

1. **Documents and steps.** A list of what is needed to register a program and what is needed to register each apprentice, including the DAS 1, and who on Mesa Valley's side owns each item.
2. **Jurisdiction.** Which advisory body likely has jurisdiction, your confidence, and the reason.
3. **Timeline.** A phased timeline range for each route (join and register new), with the drivers named and at least one action Mesa Valley controls to move faster.
4. **Register-or-join recommendation.** A clear recommendation with reasons tied to the decision factors, plus the questions you would ask the intermediary.
5. **Next steps.** Three to five dated next steps, including who you will connect Mesa Valley with.
6. **WIOA note.** One short paragraph on what you will check for the two WIOA participants, marked against your board's local policy.

## Constraints

- Do not quote exact regulatory review periods; refer to 8 CCR 206 and say you will confirm with DAS.
- Attribute any "about six months" figure to the intermediary studies cited in the course catalog.
- Do not promise a start date.
- Do not assume the intermediary is accepting new employers or that the college course qualifies as RSI; flag both as items to confirm.
- Write in plain language for an HR director, not for workforce staff.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Every requirement above appears as its own labeled section.
- [ ] Program registration and apprentice registration are clearly separated.
- [ ] Jurisdiction includes a confidence level and a reason.
- [ ] The timeline is a range for each route, not a single date.
- [ ] The recommendation is justified with at least three decision factors.
- [ ] Every local-policy or unverified detail is marked "confirm with DAS" or "per [your board's] local policy."
- [ ] A colleague could read it and explain the plan to Lena without you.

## Hints

- Start with the register-or-join question. It changes every other section.
- Mesa Valley's ongoing need for about 6 apprentices a year could argue for its own program later, even if joining is the right move this fall. A two-stage recommendation is acceptable.
- Reuse your Lesson 2 checklist and your Lesson 4 timeline script.
- Read your roadmap aloud. If any sentence sounds like a guarantee, rewrite it.
