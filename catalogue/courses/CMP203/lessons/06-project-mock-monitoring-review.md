## Goal

Run a small mock monitoring review of a fictional provider and produce the work products a real review would leave behind: a file checklist, internal sample results, policy-communication evidence, and a corrective action plan.

## The case

Eastgate Career Services is a subrecipient of the fictional Valley Regional Workforce Board, serving WIOA Title I adults and dislocated workers. Its next local monitoring visit is in six weeks. The program manager has asked you to run an internal review now. You have:

- Six de-identified participant files (your facilitator provides them, or use de-identified files from your own office with permission).
- The board's local supportive services policy and Eastgate's file maintenance procedure.
- A current staff list, a training sign-in sheet, and two staff acknowledgment forms for the supportive services policy.

## Requirements

1. **File checklist.** Build a one-page checklist covering eligibility, data agreement with CalJOBS, assessment and plan, training justification, supportive services, EO acknowledgment, case notes, and exit. Name a source for every line.
2. **Sample results.** Review all six files against your checklist. Produce a summary table showing pass/fail by checklist item and an overall list of findings and observations.
3. **Monitor's view.** For each finding, note which kind of monitor (local, state, or EO) would most likely raise it and why.
4. **Policy-communication evidence.** Assess whether the sign-in sheet and acknowledgments prove the supportive services policy reached all staff. List any gaps.
5. **Corrective action plan.** Write a CAP for the most significant finding, with finding, root cause, correction, systemic fix, and verification, each with an owner and date.

## Constraints

- Use only fictional or de-identified information. No real names, SSNs, or case data.
- Cite directives and policies only by name; mark anything you would need to confirm against the current WSD24-11, state guidance, or your board's policy as "confirm."
- Keep the full package to no more than five pages.

## Definition of done

- Every checklist line has a source or is labeled "local best practice."
- Every finding is supported by a specific file and item.
- The CAP's root cause is a process, not "staff error," and its verification step uses your sampling routine.
- A peer reviewer, acting as the monitor, agrees the CAP would close the finding.

## Hints

- Look for patterns across the six files before writing findings; repeated misses point to a process problem.
- Check dates: documents dated after the eligibility determination and exit dates that do not match the last service are common findings.
- For the policy-communication evidence, compare the names on the sign-in sheet with the staff list.
