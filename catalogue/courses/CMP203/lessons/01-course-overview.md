## Description

Monitoring visits tend to arrive as a scramble: pull the files, find the signed policies, and hope nothing important is missing. This course helps you run your office so that a monitor could walk in on any ordinary Tuesday and find the same well-kept files and documented procedures they would see after a week of preparation.

You will look at participant files and written procedures the way local, state, and equal opportunity monitors do, then build the internal routines that catch problems early. You will also practice proving that staff actually received and followed local policy, and writing corrective action plans that close findings instead of repeating them.

The course is built for supervisors, quality assurance staff, and subrecipient managers. Bring a sense of your own board's local monitoring policy and a few de-identified file examples; the activities work best when you can apply them to your own operation.

## Objectives

- Describe what local, state, and EO monitors review in participant files and procedures.
- Build a file checklist and a sampling routine for internal quality review.
- Show evidence that local policies were communicated to and implemented by staff.
- Write a corrective action plan that closes a finding.
