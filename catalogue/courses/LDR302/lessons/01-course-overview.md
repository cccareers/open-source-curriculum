## Description

AJCC certification and local plan modifications land on the same desks in the same season, and both ask the same hard question: can you show, with evidence, that the people serving customers in your centers are well trained? This half-day workshop is built for board staff, operators, and managers who have to answer that question on a deadline.

You will work through the certification indicators in California's current directive, then go deep on indicator #6, "high-quality, well-informed, and cross-trained staff." You will practice turning ordinary training records into a credible evidence package, writing the staff-training narrative that local plan modifications due April 30, 2027 call for, and converting certification findings into concrete training actions.

You leave with working drafts, not just notes: an indicator #6 evidence package, plan language you can adapt, and a Continuous Improvement Plan outline. Every policy detail in this course points you back to the directive and your board's local policy, so bring a copy of WSD25-05 and your board's own certification and planning materials.

## Objectives

- Interpret the seven certification indicators under WSD25-05 and what evidence each needs.
- Assemble a cross-training evidence package for indicator #6.
- Draft the frontline staff training section of a local plan modification.
- Turn certification findings into a Continuous Improvement Plan with training actions.
