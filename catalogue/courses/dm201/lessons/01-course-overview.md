## Description

Search is the one channel where people tell you exactly what they want, in their own words, at the moment they want it. This course teaches you how a search engine decides which pages are allowed to answer, and how to make sure yours is one of them. You will start with the machinery — how pages are found, stored, and chosen — because every tactic that follows only makes sense once you know which stage of that pipeline it acts on.

From there the work moves outward in scope. You will learn to read a query and tell what the person behind it actually wants, turn research data into a prioritized plan, and then implement that plan on a real page, across a whole site structure, and down into the technical layer that decides whether any of it is visible at all. You will publish the result in a content management system, plan an honest way to earn attention and links from other sites, and measure what happened in Google Search Console and analytics.

This is a hands-on course. At eighty hours it is the longest in the pathway, and most of that time is spent on live sites and real data rather than on theory. Expect to produce artifacts you could hand to a client: a keyword map, a competitor gap list, an on-page specification, a technical findings table, and a measurement memo. The final project pulls all of it together into a full audit and a ninety-day plan you defend out loud.

## Objectives

- Explain how crawling, indexing, and ranking determine whether a page can appear in search results
- Classify the search intent behind a query and match it to an appropriate page type
- Build a prioritized keyword map from research data
- Analyze competing sites to find realistic ranking opportunities
- Optimize a page's title, headings, metadata, and body copy for a target query
- Design a site structure and internal linking plan that distributes authority
- Diagnose crawlability, speed, and mobile issues that suppress rankings
- Publish and optimize SEO content in a content management system
- Plan an ethical off-page and link acquisition strategy
- Measure organic performance in Search Console and analytics and act on what it shows

## Prerequisite Knowledge

- dm101 Introduction to Digital Marketing, or equivalent familiarity with digital marketing channels
- Comfortable editing page content and metadata in a website or CMS
