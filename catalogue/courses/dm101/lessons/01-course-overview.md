## Description

Digital marketing is a field made of specialties — search, social, email, analytics, advertising — and it is very hard to choose between them before you know what each one is for. This course gives you the map. It surveys the major channels, the vocabulary practitioners actually use, and the judgment that decides which channel fits which job.

You will work at survey depth on purpose. Each channel gets a single lesson here; the courses that follow take them to working depth one at a time. What you build in this course is the connective tissue between them — how a message gets defined before it gets distributed, how a channel maps to a stage of someone's decision, how you tell whether any of it worked, and what the law and the ad platforms permit you to do.

You will finish by writing an integrated plan for a real business: positioning, copy, a channel mix, a measurement plan, and a compliance review. That is close to the first real document a working marketer gets asked for, and by the end of this course you will be able to defend every line of it.

## Objectives

- Describe the main digital marketing channels and how they map to stages of a customer journey
- Write a positioning statement and on-brand messaging for a defined audience
- Explain how search engines decide which pages to rank
- Plan an organic social presence and respond to audience conversation appropriately
- Design a basic email lifecycle sequence for a new subscriber
- Select the right KPIs for a campaign goal and read a basic performance report
- Apply data-privacy and advertising-policy requirements to a marketing plan
