## Description

By now you can build an automation. You can wire a trigger to a few actions, put an AI step in the middle, and get a result out the other end. This course is about the harder question that comes next: which work in a real business is worth automating, and what has to be true for the automation to survive contact with the people who depend on it.

You will spend most of your time on processes rather than platforms. You will map a real workflow before you touch a builder, express that map as triggers, actions, and durable state, and then build the four applications that cover most back-office automation demand: moving and cleaning data, producing reports, generating and reading documents, and handling inbound customer messages. Every customer-facing automation you build here keeps a human in the loop, because a workflow that can send something to a customer without a person seeing it first is a liability, not a feature.

The last stretch of the course is about what happens after launch. You will plan a rollout that the affected team will actually accept, diagnose failures from execution logs instead of guesswork, and refine a working automation for reliability, latency, and cost while proving the improvement with numbers. The course closes with a small end-to-end build so you can show one complete process running with monitoring, escalation, and a way to turn it off.

## Objectives

- Map an existing business process end to end and identify which steps are worth automating
- Design a workflow's triggers, actions, and state so it runs correctly across systems and repeated executions
- Build a workflow that ingests, cleans, and routes business data without manual handling
- Produce an automated report that assembles data, adds AI-generated narrative, and delivers on a schedule
- Generate documents from structured data using templates and AI-written sections
- Extract and summarize the contents of inbound documents into structured fields a workflow can act on
- Build an email triage workflow that classifies, routes, and drafts responses under human review
- Design a customer-service automation with explicit confidence thresholds and escalation to a person
- Integrate an AI workflow into an existing operation with a rollout plan people will actually adopt
- Diagnose an automation failure from logs and execution history and repair the underlying cause
- Refine a working automation for reliability, latency, and cost, and prove the improvement
- Deliver an end-to-end automated business process with monitoring, escalation, and a rollback path

## Prerequisite Knowledge

- Completion of ai101, or equivalent prompting and evaluation skill
