## Description

When an AJCC operator or career services contract changes hands, the participants stay but the people who knew their stories often leave. Case notes go unwritten, files sit in boxes, open training and OJT agreements lose their owner, and performance data quietly slips during the weeks when nobody is sure who is responsible.

This half-day workshop is for you whether you sit at the board, run the outgoing provider, or lead the incoming one. Drawing on LA County's 2024 transition as the backdrop, you will work through how to hand off caseloads and knowledge deliberately, how to spot which outcomes are most exposed during the gap, how to get new staff productive fast, and how to make the next transition easier by writing expectations into the procurement and contract itself.

You will leave with a working draft of the documents you need, built around a fictional transition you can adapt to your own local area.

## Objectives

- Build a transition plan covering caseloads, files, open contracts, and data entry.
- Identify the performance indicators most at risk during a transition.
- Set up a rapid onboarding sequence for incoming staff.
- Write transition and knowledge-transfer requirements into RFPs and contracts.
