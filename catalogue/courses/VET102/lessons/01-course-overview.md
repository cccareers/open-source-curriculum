## Description

A service member who walks into your AJCC six months before separation, or a military spouse who just followed a permanent change of station to your county, is often eligible for more than they realize. Whether they get it depends on the first few routing decisions you make: whether you recognize their eligibility early, give them priority they are entitled to, send them to the right staff, and check whether the training they want can also draw on their GI Bill.

This live-virtual session gives you a routing protocol you can use on Monday. You will work through the eligibility logic for separating service members and spouses, practice applying priority of service wherever a customer first touches your system, sort out who serves whom between Title I and JVSG staff, and learn how to confirm GI Bill approval for an apprenticeship or OJT program. Along the way you will see why co-enrollment matters so the participant's outcomes count.

Expect short discussion prompts and quick case activities throughout. Every directive is cited by number so you know where to look; always confirm the current version and your board's local policy before you act.

## Objectives

- Determine Dislocated Worker eligibility for a separating service member or military spouse (20 CFR 680.660, TEGL 19-16).
- Apply veterans' priority of service at every point of entry.
- Refer correctly between Title I and JVSG staff under TEGL 03-24.
- Check whether an apprenticeship or OJT program is GI Bill approved and explain the benefit to the veteran.
