## Two programs, two staff roles, one customer

The Jobs for Veterans State Grants (JVSG) program funds two staff roles that you will find in or near most California AJCCs, usually employed by EDD:

- **Disabled Veterans' Outreach Program (DVOP) specialists** provide individualized career services to veterans and eligible persons who have a significant barrier to employment (SBE), plus any additional populations DOL designates.
- **Local Veterans' Employment Representatives (LVERs)** work with employers: outreach, job development, and advocating for hiring veterans. LVERs do not carry a caseload of individual veterans.

Title I staff serve veterans every day, and most veterans who walk in should be served by Title I and other one-stop partners just like any other customer, with priority of service. JVSG staff are a specialized resource. TEGL 03-24 is the current federal guidance on how these roles are used; your referral decisions should follow it, not habit.

The most common errors are opposite mistakes: sending **every** veteran to the DVOP ("they're the veterans person"), or never sending anyone because the Title I counselor wants to keep the case.

## The referral decision

Use this sequence each time a covered person is identified:

1. **Serve first, screen early.** Priority of service applies (previous lesson). Do not hold a veteran at the front desk waiting for a DVOP.
2. **Screen for SBE.** Use your AJCC's current screening form. The qualifying conditions and the additional designated populations are listed in TEGL 03-24 and your state's implementing guidance. Categories such as a service-connected disability, homelessness, recent release from incarceration, and lacking a high school diploma or equivalent have long appeared on the list; **confirm the current list before you rely on it.**
3. **Check JVSG eligibility.** JVSG uses its own definitions of "eligible veteran" and "eligible person," which are narrower than the priority of service definition. Check them in the guidance.
4. **Route:**

| Screening result | Route |
| --- | --- |
| SBE present (or designated population) and JVSG-eligible | Refer to DVOP for individualized career services; Title I may co-enroll for training funds. |
| No SBE | Serve through Title I and partners with priority of service. Do not refer to DVOP for case management. |
| Customer is an employer, or a veteran needs employer connections | Involve the LVER for employer-side work. |
| DVOP not available at the site | Title I serves the customer now; follow local procedure for a later or virtual DVOP connection. |

5. **Document the screening and the referral** in CalJOBS and the case file, including when no referral was made and why.

## Co-enrollment so the outcomes count

A veteran working with a DVOP who also needs an ITA or OJT is often **co-enrolled** in Title I. Co-enrollment lets each program provide what it is funded to provide and makes sure the participant's employment and credential outcomes are recorded where they belong. Without it, the training Title I paid for may not show up in Title I performance, and the DVOP's services may not show up in JVSG reporting.

Practical rules of thumb:

- Agree who is the lead case manager and write it in the case notes.
- Share the plan; do not build two competing employment plans.
- Check your board's co-enrollment policy for when and how to enroll in each program.

### Worked example

**Carlos Rivera**, an Army veteran, recently left a transitional housing program and wants welding training. Screening shows he was recently homeless, which is a listed SBE category in your current form. He meets JVSG eligibility.

```text
SBE screening completed [date]; customer reports homelessness within [period
per form]. JVSG eligibility confirmed. Referred to DVOP [name] for
individualized career services; warm handoff completed same day. Title I
co-enrollment for ITA (welding) to be determined jointly. Lead case manager:
DVOP. Title I contact: [name].
```

**Priya Shah**, Air Force veteran, recently separated, no SBE on screening, wants an IT certification. Title I serves her directly with priority of service. No DVOP referral. If an employer partner is hiring for IT roles, the LVER can help make the connection.
