## Goal

Make and document the routing decisions for three fictional customers who arrive at Valley Regional AJCC in the same week. For each one, decide Dislocated Worker eligibility, apply priority of service, decide whether a JVSG referral is appropriate, and check whether the training they want can use GI Bill benefits. Your work product is a short routing memo a supervisor or monitor could follow.

## The customers

**1. Staff Sgt. Elena Ruiz, Army.** Separating in four months with orders showing an expected honorable discharge. Wants a registered electrical apprenticeship with Valley Electrical JATC. Reports no housing, disability, or income barriers.

**2. Tomas Nguyen, spouse of a Navy petty officer.** Lost his pharmacy technician job in another state when the family received PCS orders to a base in your region three months ago. Not a veteran himself. Wants a short pharmacy tech refresher and job placement. The Adult ITA list for that program has one open slot and two other eligible applicants.

**3. Jordan Blake, Marine Corps veteran.** Separated two years ago with an honorable discharge. Has a service-connected disability rating and has been couch-surfing for six weeks. Interested in an OJT position with a local HVAC company.

## Requirements

For each customer, your memo must state:

1. **DW eligibility:** which category applies (or why none does), the facts that must be documented, and what can be collected now versus later.
2. **Priority of service:** whether the customer is a covered person, and how that affects their place for services or limited slots.
3. **Referral:** SBE screening result, whether to refer to a DVOP, any LVER involvement, and the co-enrollment plan with a named lead case manager.
4. **GI Bill check:** whether the customer could use GI Bill benefits, how you checked (or would check) program approval, and the plain-language explanation you would give.
5. **A sample case note** of no more than six lines.

## Constraints

- Cite directives only by the numbers used in this course (20 CFR 680.660, TEGL 19-16, TEGL 03-24, 20 CFR 680.730). Where a detail depends on a list or threshold, write "confirm in [directive or local policy]" rather than stating it.
- Do not quote GI Bill dollar amounts or percentages.
- Treat your board's local policy as a variable; name where it would apply.
- Use only the fictional facts given. If a fact you need is missing, state the question you would ask.

## Definition of Done

- All three customers have a decision in each of the four areas, with a one-sentence reason.
- At least one decision for each customer points to the document you would confirm it against.
- Your memo correctly identifies which customers are not covered persons for priority of service or not JVSG-eligible, if any, and explains why.
- Each case note names the next action and a follow-up date placeholder.

## Hints

- Tomas is a spouse, not a veteran. Check both the DW spouse category and the eligible spouse definition for priority of service; they are separate questions with possibly different answers.
- Elena has no SBE. Ask yourself whether a DVOP referral is the right move.
- Jordan may qualify for several things at once. Decide who leads the case before deciding who funds the OJT.
- A GI Bill check is about the specific employer program, not the trade.
