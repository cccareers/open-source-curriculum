## Description

Every SaaS deal you work has two sets of numbers attached to it. There are your company's numbers — what the contract is worth, what it cost to win, how long before that cost is paid back, whether the account grows or shrinks after signature. And there are the customer's numbers — what their current process costs them, what they lose to the problem you fix, what they would gain if it went away. Reps who can only talk about features are guessing at both. This course is about learning to read and argue with both sets.

You will start with the shape of the revenue itself, because a subscription deal, a usage-based deal, and a hybrid deal with the same headline price are not worth the same thing to your company or to the buyer. From there you will build the standard SaaS unit-economics vocabulary — acquisition cost, lifetime value, payback period — by calculating each one by hand from account data, and then the retention vocabulary that tells you whether a book of business is quietly growing or quietly leaking. None of these are finance-department trivia. They decide which accounts are worth your week.

The second half turns the lens around. You will take a prospect's own cost and revenue figures, build a defensible baseline and a conservative future state, and frame the deal in the buyer's language rather than yours. Then you will plan the year after the signature — the touchpoints, the stakeholder coverage, and the expansion paths that keep an account from becoming next year's churn number. The course closes with a project that asks you to produce the artifact all of it points at: a business case a finance leader would actually sign off on.

## Objectives

- Explain how subscription, usage-based, and hybrid revenue models change what a SaaS deal is worth
- Calculate customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, and payback period from account data
- Interpret ARR, net revenue retention, and churn to judge the health of a book of business
- Frame a deal using the prospect's own cost and revenue numbers
- Design a retention and expansion plan for an existing account
