## Description

Most content that gets published should never have been made. Someone had a good idea in a meeting, someone else wrote it, and it went out into a world where nobody was looking for it. This course is about the opposite habit: deciding what to make from evidence about real people, making it well, putting it somewhere a reader can actually reach, getting it in front of them, and then checking whether it did anything.

You will work through a single running brand — a small direct-to-consumer gardening company — from audience research to a published, distributed, measured piece. Along the way you will write and edit real copy, plan and shoot a short how-to video with captions and a transcript, model a content library in a CMS so it stays usable after the two-hundredth post, and read a performance table closely enough to defend what you would publish next.

Accessibility is treated here as part of the craft rather than a compliance step at the end. Alt text, captions, transcripts, link text, and heading structure are content decisions, and you will make them in the same pass where you make every other content decision.

## Objectives

- Build a content strategy from audience research and business goals
- Write clear, persuasive marketing copy for digital channels
- Plan and produce multimedia content appropriate to a channel
- Publish, structure, and maintain content in a CMS
- Distribute and repurpose one piece of content across multiple channels
- Measure content performance and decide what to publish next
