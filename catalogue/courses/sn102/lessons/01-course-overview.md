## Description

ServiceNow is a cloud platform that organizations use to run their work: an IT team logs and resolves outages on it, an HR team answers benefits questions on it, a customer support team tracks cases on it, and a risk team keeps evidence of controls on it. All of that sits on one shared foundation of tables, records, and automation. This course is your first look at that foundation, and it assumes you have never signed in to an instance before.

You will start with what an instance actually is and how the platform is put together, then learn to move around it the way an implementer does — lists, forms, filters, and the settings you will adjust every day. From there you will look underneath the interface at the data model, because almost everything you will ever build on this platform is a table, a record, and a rule about who may touch it. Access control follows immediately, since roles decide what every later application will let a person see.

The last stretch of the course is a guided tour. You will meet the core IT Service Management processes and the records they create, then look at Customer Service Management, HR Service Delivery, and Governance, Risk, and Compliance to understand which business problem each one solves. Finally you will see how workflow automation carries a record from request to completion without anyone forwarding an email. You will finish by setting up your own personal developer instance so that every course after this one has somewhere to practice.

## Objectives

- Describe the ServiceNow platform architecture and how instances, applications, and the core data model fit together
- Navigate an instance and configure the system settings, lists, and preferences an implementer uses daily
- Explain how tables, records, fields, and table extension organize data on the platform
- Explain how users, groups, roles, and access controls determine who can see and change a record
- Identify the core ITSM processes and the records each one creates
- Describe what the CSM, HRSD, and GRC applications do and which business problem each one solves
- Explain how workflow automation moves work through the platform without manual handoffs
