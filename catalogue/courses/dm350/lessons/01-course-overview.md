## Description

A CRM is where a marketing team stops guessing. Every form submission, every email opened, every call an advisor logged lands in one place as a record you can filter, segment, and act on. This course teaches you to build that place well enough that automation running on top of it does the right thing instead of the embarrassing thing.

You will work on one fictional business for the whole course, so the numbers stay real to you rather than staying illustrations. You will model its customer data, build the path that turns an anonymous visitor into a known contact, write and send email to those contacts, split them into segments that deserve different messages, and then hand the whole sequence over to a workflow that runs without you. When the email starts landing in spam folders, you will have the arithmetic to find out why.

This is the capstone of the pathway, so it assumes you can already read a conversion report and talk about a landing page without flinching. It closes by asking what you owe the people in your database: consent you can prove, retention you can justify, and a deletion request you can actually honor. The final project asks you to build one funnel end to end and defend every decision in it.

## Objectives

- Model contacts, companies, and activities in a CRM so the data stays usable
- Design a lead capture path and qualify the leads it produces
- Design an email campaign with a clear goal, structure, and call to action
- Segment an audience and personalize content for each segment
- Build an automated nurture workflow with entry criteria, branching, and exits
- Diagnose email deliverability and performance problems and fix them
- Apply an AI-driven or conversational martech capability to a marketing task
- Handle consent, retention, and data-subject requests in a CRM lawfully
