## Description

Every organization you will implement for is already under obligation to somebody. A regulator, a customer contract, an internal security policy, an insurer, a board committee. Governance, risk, and compliance work is the discipline of turning those obligations into something an organization can actually operate: named policies, testable controls, owned risks, and evidence a stranger can audit. This course teaches you to build that machinery on the ServiceNow platform using the Integrated Risk Management applications.

You will start by learning what the applications are for and how they fit together, then work through the compliance side first, because it is the concrete half. Authority documents, citations, policies, control objectives, and controls are records you can point at, and once you can model them you have something to attach the more abstract risk register to. From there you will scope programs to real assets using the CMDB, test controls continuously with indicators instead of once a year with a spreadsheet, automate policy review and risk acceptance with Flow Designer, and route control failures into issues, remediation tasks, and audit engagements.

The course closes with reporting, because the visible output of a GRC program is an executive view of posture, and with a project that asks you to stand up a small but complete compliance program end to end. Throughout, you are learning the platform's implementation of GRC, not the frameworks themselves. Authority documents such as SOX, ISO 27001, and the NIST catalogs show up here as data you load and map, never as material you are expected to memorize.

## Objectives

- Explain what an integrated risk management program does and which platform applications support it
- Model authority documents, citations, policies, and control objectives in the compliance application
- Configure controls and attestations that produce audit-ready evidence on a schedule
- Build a risk register with a scoring methodology and run a risk assessment against it
- Scope a compliance or risk program to the right entities using CMDB data
- Configure indicators that test control effectiveness continuously instead of once a year
- Automate policy review, risk acceptance, and attestation campaigns with Flow Designer
- Route a failed control into an issue and a remediation task, and support an audit engagement
- Build risk and compliance dashboards that perform well and expose data only to authorized readers
