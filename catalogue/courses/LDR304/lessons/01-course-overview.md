## Description

Every board says it supports apprenticeship. Far fewer have decided how far in they are actually going. This briefing is for executive directors, board members, and program directors who need to make that decision deliberately, with their own numbers, staff, and budget in front of them.

You will look honestly at what your board does with apprenticeship today, choose the level of involvement your resources can support, set a co-enrollment goal you can staff, and identify the state and federal money that can pay for it. Along the way you will see how TEGL 05-25, its 10 percent co-enrollment goal, and the pending federal reauthorization shape the decision, and where to check the current guidance before you commit.

You will leave with a one-page strategy you can take to your board or leadership team.

## Objectives

- Assess their board's current apprenticeship capacity and outcomes.
- Choose a role (referrer, navigator, intermediary, or sponsor) that fits their resources.
- Set a co-enrollment goal and the staffing and training needed to meet it.
- Identify the state and federal funding that can pay for the strategy.

## Prerequisite Knowledge
