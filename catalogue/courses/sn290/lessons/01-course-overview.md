## Description

Most people who use ServiceNow never see the platform you have been learning. They see a portal — a search box, a few cards, a catalog of things they can request, and a list of what they already asked for. Everything behind that page is the work you already know how to do; the portal is the part the organization actually judges you on. This course is about building that surface well.

You will work in Service Portal, the framework built from portal records, pages, containers, rows, columns, and widgets. Widgets are where the real craft lives: each one is a small bundle of HTML, CSS, an AngularJS client controller, and a server script, and once you understand how those four pieces talk to each other you can build almost anything a stakeholder asks for. Along the way you will make the layout survive a phone screen, wire up navigation someone can actually follow, and surface Knowledge and the Service Catalog with permissions that hold.

The last stretch is discipline rather than construction. You will learn to find out why a page takes four seconds to paint, and to check a portal against the accessibility requirements that most public-sector and enterprise buyers now write into contracts. The course ends with a project: one branded portal, built by you, that a stranger could use without instructions.

## Objectives

- Assemble a Service Portal from portal records, pages, containers, rows, and columns
- Build and clone widgets using HTML, CSS, an AngularJS client controller, and a server script
- Lay out a portal that works on a phone and give it navigation a user can follow
- Surface the Knowledge Base and Service Catalog through the portal with the correct permissions
- Diagnose a slow portal page and meet baseline accessibility requirements
