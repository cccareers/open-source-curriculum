## Description

Most deals are not lost in the negotiation. They are lost weeks earlier, in a discovery call where nobody established what the problem was actually costing, and then they are formally buried at the end when a representative with nothing to stand on trades margin for a signature. This course treats negotiation as the last visible stage of a much longer chain, and it teaches you the whole chain — how to run a disciplined consultative conversation, how to hear an objection accurately before you answer it, how to change how a buyer sees their own situation, and only then how to handle the commercial conversation about money and terms.

The commercial work here is B2B software-subscription shaped: contract term, seat counts, ramp schedules, payment timing, discount approval limits. You will plan anchors and concession ladders before you ever open your mouth, learn which non-price terms are cheap for you and valuable to the buyer, and practice giving nothing away without getting something back. Legal drafting is deliberately not your job — you will learn where the line sits between a commercial term you negotiate and contract language you route to your legal team, and how to hold that line politely under pressure.

Expect to be on your feet. Nearly every session ends with a live exercise built on a realistic buyer, and the pattern is always the same: prepare on paper, run it out loud with a partner playing someone unhelpful, then review what you actually said against what you planned to say. Negotiation is a performance skill under time pressure, and the gap between knowing a tactic and executing it while a procurement manager is staring at you only closes with reps.

## Objectives

- Run a SPIN-structured discovery conversation that surfaces implication and need-payoff
- Apply the Sandler method's rapport-building, up-front contract, and pain funnel in a live conversation
- Diagnose an objection before answering it, and choose a response that keeps the deal on merit
- Deliver a commercial insight that reframes how the buyer sees their own situation
- Plan anchors, concessions, and trades for a pricing discussion before it starts
- Negotiate non-price contract terms without giving away margin
- Close on a mutual action plan with named owners and dates

## Prerequisite Knowledge

- Introduction to Sales (se101), or equivalent sales fundamentals
- Advanced SaaS Sales Strategies (se201), for the pricing and unit-economics vocabulary
