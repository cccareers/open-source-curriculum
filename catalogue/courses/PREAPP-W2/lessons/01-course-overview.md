## Description

Last week you proved you can send. Your CRM is live, fifty or more contacts are initiated, and your profile is public. This week the question changes from "did you send?" to "did it work, and why?" Volume alone stops paying off fast. What pays off from here is a system: knowing exactly who is worth your outreach hours, reaching more than one person inside the organizations that matter most, and letting real reply data — not your gut — decide which version of your message survives.

You will spend this week in the lab building that system on your own live pipeline. You will write an ideal contact profile and use it to qualify and disqualify the names already sitting in your CRM. You will multi-thread your priority employer-partner accounts by mapping two to three relevant contacts inside each one. You will split your sends into two message variants, log replies against each, and read the results honestly at the small sample sizes you actually have. And you will take the replies that come back and turn them into confirmed calendar events instead of friendly threads that quietly die.

The week ends with a hard milestone, and it is not a document: two or more conversations booked, and your first informational interview completed and debriefed. Everything in these lab guides exists to get you to that outcome with a repeatable method behind it, so that week three starts from a system you trust rather than a lucky reply.

## Objectives

- Define ideal contact profiles and qualify prospects, mapping multiple contacts per priority organization
- A/B test message variants against real reply data and adapt tone and ask by audience
- Convert replies into scheduled conversations and run structured informational interviews
