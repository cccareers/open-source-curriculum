## What priority of service is for

Funds for individualized career services and training under the WIOA Adult program are limited. Priority of service decides who goes first when a choice has to be made. It doesn't decide who is *eligible*. A person can be fully eligible for the Adult program and still be in a lower priority group.

Two things go wrong in files:

1. Staff apply the order inconsistently, so two similar people get different treatment.
2. Staff never **record** the person's priority status at eligibility, so a monitor can't tell whether the order was followed.

This lesson fixes both. Your references are the state directives on priority of service, WSD24-06 and WSD24-15, plus your board's local policy. Read the current versions before you apply anything here.

## The building blocks

Priority of service combines two separate sets of status:

- **Veterans and eligible spouses.** Covered persons receive priority under federal veterans' priority rules.
- **WIOA Adult statutory priority groups.** Recipients of public assistance, other low-income individuals, and individuals who are basic skills deficient.

Your board may also add **local priority groups**. Where those sit in the order is set by the state directives and your local policy, so check both.

The order is commonly laid out in tiers, which combine the two sets:

| Tier | Who (confirm against WSD24-06 / WSD24-15) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Veterans and eligible spouses who are also in a WIOA Adult priority group |
| 2 | Individuals who are not veterans or eligible spouses but are in a WIOA Adult priority group |
| 3 | Veterans and eligible spouses who are not in a WIOA Adult priority group |
| 4 | Others, including any local priority groups as your directives and local policy define |

Use this table as a thinking aid. The directives are what bind you. If the current WSD24-06 or WSD24-15 describes the order differently, follow the directive and correct your notes.

Priority of service applies to the Adult program. Dislocated Worker and Youth eligibility work differently. Don't apply the Adult tiers to them unless a directive or local policy tells you to.

## Recording status at eligibility

The determination happens at eligibility, using the same evidence you already collected. For each Adult enrollee, record:

- Veteran or eligible spouse status: yes or no, and the document
- Each statutory priority group that applies, and its evidence (public assistance record, low-income calculation, basic skills assessment result)
- Any local priority group, with the local policy cited
- The resulting tier
- The date determined

If your case management system has fields for these, fill them in. A tier in the system with no supporting evidence in the file is a finding waiting to happen.

## Worked example: two applicants, one training slot

Valley Regional Workforce Board has one remaining training slot this quarter for a phlebotomy cohort.

| | Luis | Danielle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Veteran | Yes (DD-214 on file) | No |
| Public assistance | No | Yes, CalFresh (county verification) |
| Low income | No (calculated, over threshold) | Yes |
| Basic skills deficient | No (assessment on file) | No |
| Tier | 3 | 2 |

Danielle is in a statutory priority group and Luis isn't, so under the tier order above she has priority for the Adult-funded training slot. Luis is still eligible and should be offered other services, or the next slot, or co-enrollment options your board offers. The case note for each names the tier and the evidence. Confirm this result against the current directives before using it as a pattern.

**Discussion prompt:** Many staff assume a veteran always goes first. Why does that assumption fail here, and where in the directive would you show a colleague the answer?

## Common errors to avoid

- Deciding priority at the training referral instead of at eligibility
- Recording "low income: yes" with no calculation in the file
- Treating "veteran" as the whole answer instead of one of two status sets
- Applying a local priority group that isn't in the board's written policy
