## The funding question

Danielle has chosen the teacher apprenticeship. Her next question is the one every participant asks: "Who pays for the college part?" Your answer depends on how the program is built, which part of it you are being asked to fund, and your board's local policy. This lesson gives you the decision logic.

## The building blocks

A degree-linked apprenticeship usually has up to three fundable parts. Each one connects to a different WIOA tool.

| Part of the program | WIOA tool that may apply | Key rule to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-the-job learning with the employer | On-the-job training (OJT) contract with the employer | OJT reimbursement rule at 20 CFR 680.730 and your board's OJT policy |
| Related instruction that is part of the registered program | Individual Training Account (ITA) | The program must be on the ETPL |
| Degree coursework outside the registered program (for example, a later bachelor's phase at a different institution) | ITA, if that program is itself on the ETPL | Separate ETPL listing for that specific program |
| Barriers to participation, such as transportation or books | Supportive services | Your board's supportive services policy |

Other sources come first in the conversation. The employer or sponsor may already cover related instruction, and the participant may qualify for financial aid such as a Pell Grant. WIOA expects you to coordinate ITA funds with other grant aid rather than replace it, so ask about financial aid early. Check your board's local policy for how it handles coordination with Pell and other aid.

## The ETPL and registered apprenticeships

ITAs can only pay for training on the Eligible Training Provider List. Registered apprenticeship is treated differently from other training on the list. CCC's catalog states that under WSD25-02, California's ETPL directive, every registered apprenticeship program is automatically eligible for the ETPL. APP 101 covers why.

For a degree-linked apprenticeship, the practical questions are:

1. **Is the registered program on the list?** Automatic eligibility is not the same as already being listed. Check the ETPL in CalJOBS and confirm in WSD25-02 how registered programs are added.
2. **Is the college coursework part of that registered program?** If the degree courses are the program's related instruction, they travel with the registered program.
3. **Is any part of the degree outside the registered program?** If so, that part needs its own ETPL listing before an ITA can pay for it. A university program that is "a partner" is not automatically on the list.

Directive numbers here come from the course catalog. Open the current WSD25-02 on EDD's directives page before you train others on it, in case it has been revised.

## Eligibility and need

Funding does not start with the program. It starts with the participant.

- **Program eligibility.** The participant must be eligible for Adult, Dislocated Worker, or Youth services, documented according to state guidance.
- **Need for training.** Training services require a determination that the participant needs training to get or keep employment. An apprentice already has a job, so check how your board's local policy handles training for employed participants, including its definition of self-sufficiency.
- **Plan.** The training goes in the participant's IEP or ISS, with the occupation and the credential named.

## Avoiding overlap

When you combine an OJT contract for the job and an ITA for the classroom, each dollar must pay for a different thing. OJT reimburses the employer for part of the wages during on-the-job learning; the ITA pays the training provider for instruction. Do not pay for the same hours or the same cost twice. APP 102 covers how to build the combined contract package and invoicing in detail.

## Worked example: Danielle's funding map

```text
Participant: Danielle Ortiz (fictional). Program: Teacher Apprenticeship, registered with DAS.
Eligibility: Adult; employed; meets local self-sufficiency criteria for training (per local policy).
Related instruction (Riverbend CC, associate phase): part of registered program.
  - Listed on ETPL: confirmed in CalJOBS on [date].
  - Financial aid: participant applying for Pell; ITA to cover remaining tuition and books
    up to local ITA cap.
On-the-job learning: district is a public employer; OJT not pursued (district already
  funds apprentice wages; confirm OJT policy if this changes).
Bachelor's and credential phase: university partner not yet on ETPL. No ITA commitment.
  Revisit when program is listed.
Supportive services: transportation to evening classes, per local policy.
```

The map commits funds only where every rule is confirmed and flags the part that is not yet fundable.
