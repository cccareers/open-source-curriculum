## Description

Guidance changes every program year, but desk guides, local policies, and habits often do not. This clinic is your annual refresh: a short, practical session that turns new and cancelled guidance into what you actually do differently when you sit down with a participant or an employer on Monday.

The 2026 edition covers six changes: the cancellation of TEGL 09-22 on youth flexibilities, the rescission of TEGL 10-23 Change 2 on work authorization, the new ETPL directive WSD25-02, AJCC certification under WSD25-05, the 90 percent OJT waiver, and the CalJOBS modernization. You will learn to spot where your own local documents still lean on old guidance, what each change means for frontline practice, and how to fix a procedure so it matches current rules.

You will also leave with a simple routine so next year's changes do not catch you or your team by surprise. Bring one local procedure or desk guide you use regularly; you will refresh it before the session ends.

## Objectives

- Identify local policies that still cite cancelled or superseded guidance.
- Explain each 2026 change and its effect on frontline practice.
- Update a local procedure or desk guide to match current rules.
- Set up a routine for catching future directive changes.
