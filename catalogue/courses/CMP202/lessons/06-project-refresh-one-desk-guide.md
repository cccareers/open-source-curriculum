## Goal

Produce one refreshed local procedure or desk guide with every superseded or cancelled citation found and replaced, plus a documented routine for tracking future directive changes.

## Scenario

You work at the Riverbend AJCC under the fictional Valley Regional Workforce Board. Your supervisor hands you the center's "Intake and Eligibility Desk Guide," last revised three years ago. It cites TEGL 10-23 Change 2 at the work-authorization step, describes a youth "flexibility" with no source, and walks through CalJOBS with screenshots. A state monitoring visit is on the calendar. You may use this scenario or, better, a real desk guide from your own office with any participant information removed.

## Requirements

1. **Citation log.** Every TEGL, WSD, WSIN, and CFR citation in the document, plus any unsourced "flexibility" or "recent guidance" phrases, with current status and action for each.
2. **Refreshed document.** Stale citations replaced or steps rewritten; verb-first steps; citations at the step; local policy variables labeled.
3. **Revision block** at the top of the document.
4. **Change-tracking routine.** A short page naming sources, roles, frequencies, and a tracker with at least one sample row.

## Constraints

- Describe each 2026 change only as far as you have confirmed it in the source. No guessed dates, dollar figures, or document lists.
- Anything you cannot confirm is marked "pending policy review," not filled in.
- Use no real participant names or case data.
- Do not mark the document approved yourself; it goes to your supervisor or policy lead.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] No citation to cancelled or rescinded guidance remains as a basis for a step.
- [ ] Every step that cites a rule names the current source.
- [ ] Every local-board choice is labeled as a local policy variable.
- [ ] The revision block lists every change and a next review date.
- [ ] The routine names who watches, how often, and where changes are logged.
- [ ] A coworker can follow the refreshed section without asking you questions.

## Hints

- Start from the citation log; the rewrite goes faster when every decision is already made.
- Move CalJOBS click paths to an appendix so the modernization only forces you to replace one page.
- If a cancelled TEGL supported a whole step, ask "what rule governs this now?" before deleting it.
