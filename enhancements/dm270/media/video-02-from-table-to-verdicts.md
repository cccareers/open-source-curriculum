## Purpose
After watching, a learner can turn a content performance table into value-per-hour figures, separate rate problems from volume problems, and give each piece exactly one of the six verdicts with the number that justifies it.

## Audience and prerequisites
Apprentices who have read lesson 07 through "A content performance table, with the arithmetic". Spreadsheet access.

## Check for understanding
1. A guide has 9% signup rate on 300 sessions. Scale it? *Answer: not yet on that evidence; 300 sessions and about 27 signups is a small count, so treat the rate as unstable, distribute it a little, and re-measure.*
2. Why is time on page the wrong KPI for the depth guide? *Answer: its job is to answer a question fast; a quick answer is success, so a short time can be good.*
3. What makes "Leave alone" a decision rather than a non-decision? *Answer: it is written down with the reason and the evidence, so it is reviewable later.*
