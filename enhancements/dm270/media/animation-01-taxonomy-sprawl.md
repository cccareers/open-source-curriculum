## Concept and misconception it fixes
Misconception: tags are harmless labels, so adding one more costs nothing. Reality from lesson 05: a freeform taxonomy grows one variant at a time ("raised beds", "raised bed", "Raised Beds"), splits the library, and every term silently generates an archive page. Harvest Lane's audit found 147 tags on 63 posts, 81 used once. The animation shows the sprawl accumulating over time, the archive pages it spawns, and then the governed alternative: a few planned taxonomies with owners, fixed terms, and one term per record where the rule says so.

## Visual language (shapes, colors with color-blind-safe palette, labels)
- Posts: small document icons with titles in text.
- Tags: rounded pill labels. Freeform tags in grey outline; duplicates of the same idea share a dashed connector and an "=" marker.
- Archive pages: thin page outlines that pop out behind each tag, labelled with their URL path (e.g. `/tag/raised-beds/`).
- Governed taxonomies: four solid columns, each with a header label (Topic, Season, Skill level, Zone) and an owner badge ("Owner: Elena").
- Palette: blue (#0072B2) for governed elements, orange (#E69F00) for duplicate/stray elements, black text on off-white. Duplicates are also marked with "=" and stray single-use tags with a "1" badge, so meaning never depends on colour.

## Interaction variant (optional)
A drag-and-drop exercise: learners receive 20 of Harvest Lane's stray tags and drop each into one of the four governed taxonomies or a "retire" bin; feedback explains each placement. Exportable as H5P "Drag and drop".
