## Summary

A practical, honest outbound course with a well-maintained running example (Northwind Mechanical, Dana Ruiz, and the field-service scheduling platform run from Lesson 2 through Lesson 6) and strong compliance guardrails. Main opportunities: model the spoken skills (cold calls) with audio/video, add graded drills before the high-stakes live campaign, and close two small integrity gaps where example copy could be copied verbatim by learners (an invented customer reference, and a "re:" subject line that seems to contradict the rules).

## Depth and coverage gaps

- **No rehearsal ladder before live dials.** Lesson 4 practice jumps from five role-plays to twenty live dials. A graded drill ladder (draft se202-x01) supports "Open, navigate, and recover a cold call, including its predictable objections".
- **Email copy feedback loop.** Peer review is in Lesson 5, but there are no annotated weak examples to calibrate reviewers. A "before/after" bank of five weak cold emails rewritten would help "Write a multi-touch email sequence that asks for a specific meeting".
- **Research time-boxing.** Lesson 3 describes deep research but not how long to spend per tier. A guideline (e.g., tier A ≤ 15 min, tier B ≤ 3 min) would help learners avoid over-researching. Maps to "Use prospecting tools to research an account and its buying committee before outreach".
- **Qualification calibration.** Learners score generously. A calibration exercise (score the same three call notes independently, compare) would support "Qualify an outbound pipeline against a stated framework and decide what to work next".
- **ICP revision evidence.** The Lesson 7 debrief asks for ICP changes but Lesson 2 gives no worked example of revising an ICP from campaign data. Maps to "Define an ideal customer profile and build a qualified target list from market signals".

## Assessment ideas

- Audio quiz: ten 5-second prospect responses; learner names the objection type and the first sentence of the response.
- Email scoring rubric (subject, first line, one idea, specific ask, word count) usable for peer review.
- Calibration check: instructor-scored "gold" MEDDIC scorecards for three sample accounts; learner score must be within ±2 total.
