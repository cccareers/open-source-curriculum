## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconceptions: (1) one great email or call should be enough; (2) "call better" is a plan. The animation lays Lesson 5's two-week cadence on a timeline, shows the email and call channels reinforcing each other for one prospect (Dana), then zooms out to the four-number calling funnel (dials → connects → conversations → meetings) and shows how the step where it narrows tells you which move to fix.

## Visual language

- Horizontal timeline Day 1 to Day 16.
- Email touches: envelope icon, blue #0072B2. Calls: phone icon, orange #E69F00. Second contact / social: person icon, bluish green #009E73. Every icon also carries its text label ("Email 1 — trigger").
- Funnel: four stacked trapezoids with numbers written inside; the narrowing step gets a thick outline and a "fix this" tag.

## Interaction variant

Learner enters their own daily activity log (dials, connects, conversations, meetings); the funnel redraws and highlights the narrowest step with the matching Lesson 4 diagnosis.

## Production notes

- Funnel numbers are illustrative and must be labeled "illustrative" on screen; the lesson deliberately avoids asserting benchmark connect rates.
- Keep email and call icons at least 44 px for legibility on mobile.
