## Purpose

After watching, the learner can write a layered search in filter logic, read a hiring signal from a job posting, map five buying-committee roles with a source beside every fact, and name the committee gap to ask about on the first call.

## Audience and prerequisites

Learners who have read Lesson 3. Tool-agnostic: the screencast uses a generic "prospecting database" mock-up and a generic "professional network" mock-up, plus a real-looking (fictional) careers page. No vendor interface is shown, because interfaces change every quarter.

## Script

| 0:00 | Host webcam inset; screen shows a plain text file titled "Search logic: field-service scheduling ICP." | "Before you touch any tool, write the search down in plain text. Then any tool can run it. Here's the one we'll build for Northwind Mechanical." |
| 0:15 | Types the company layer: industry, 120–1,500 headcount, US/Canada; excludes current customers, under 120 employees, residential-only single-branch. | "Company layer. | 0:40 | Types the person layer: function Operations; seniority Director/VP/C-level; title refine "field," "service," "dispatch"; exclude "Sales Operations," "Revenue Operations," "Business Operations." | "Person layer. | 1:05 | Types the signal layer: job change in last 90 days; headcount growth > 15%; 8+ technician or dispatcher postings in 60 days; funding/acquisition in 6 months. | "Signal layer. Any one of these makes the account tier A." |

Open ten records and check which." |
| 1:50 | Opens Northwind's record: ~90 field techs (estimated, flagged "modeled"), 4 branches. | "Northwind's in the thirty-eight. Note the technician count is modeled, not observed. We'll check it." |

This one names the ERP and says the dispatch app is homegrown. That's a free technographic record, and it tells us who our technical evaluator works for." |

| 3:25 | Priya Nandal, Director of IT. Technical evaluator; source "careers page posting." | "Priya Nandal, Director of IT. The integrations posting points to her team." |

## On-screen assets and B-roll

- Generic prospecting database and professional network mock-ups (no vendor branding).
- Fictional Northwind careers page.
- Committee-map template as a download.

## Accessibility

- Captions; every typed filter is read aloud.
- Zoom to 150% on text; result counter shown as large numerals.
- "Stale" and "low confidence" flags use icon plus text.
- Transcript and the completed map provided.

## Check for understanding

1. Why exclude "Sales Operations" and "Revenue Operations"? *(Answer: an "Operations" function filter pulls them in, and they have nothing to do with dispatching technicians.)*
2. Why does Marcus Hale's Economic buyer entry carry a source but not count as qualified? *(Answer: a name from a case study is research. Qualification needs contact with him or confirmation from the champion.)*
3. What's the committee gap, and what do you do with it? *(Answer: no finance contact or approval path above the COO. It becomes a question on the first call.)*
