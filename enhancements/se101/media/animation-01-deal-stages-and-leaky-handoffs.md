## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconceptions: (1) a sale is a straight line that only moves forward; (2) the work is mostly persuasion. The animation follows the Lesson 2 worked example (Dana's 400-person logistics company) as a token moving along the stage line, shows each role picking it up, shows context "leaking" at handoffs unless a handoff note is attached, and shows the deal moving *backwards* when a new stakeholder appears.

## Visual language

- Stage line: eight labeled stations left to right (Prospecting, Qualification, Discovery, Demonstration & evaluation, Proposal, Negotiation & approval, Close, Onboarding & renewal).
- The deal: a folder icon labeled "Dana — dispatch exceptions." Inside it, small cards represent facts learned ("90 min/dispatcher/day", "peak season in 5 months", "Director approves").
- Roles: simple avatars with text labels (SDR, AE, SE, CSM on the vendor side, above the line; Dana, Director, IT, Procurement below the line).
- Palette (Okabe-Ito, color-blind safe): vendor roles blue #0072B2, customer roles orange #E69F00, leaked facts vermillion #D55E00 with a dashed outline, preserved facts bluish green #009E73 with a solid outline. Every state is also distinguished by outline style and a text label.

## Interaction variant

Step-through H5P version: learner clicks each station and must type the one question that has to be answered before moving on (mirrors Lesson 2 practice step 1). At the two handoffs, learner chooses which facts to put in the note; facts not chosen visibly fall off.
