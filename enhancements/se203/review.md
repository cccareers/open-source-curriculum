## Summary

A deep, well-sequenced course (SPIN → Sandler → diagnosis → insight → pricing → terms → close) with excellent annotated transcripts and a demanding capstone simulation. I found three arithmetic errors in the pricing/terms lessons (fixed) and a cross-lesson inconsistency in the Marcus deal's figures (flagged). The biggest opportunity is modeled audio/video of the high-pressure moments — the flinch, the silence, the revoked concession — which learners currently only read about.

## Depth and coverage gaps

- **Spoken modeling.** Silence after a price, after question 7, and after a reframe is the core skill in four lessons, and it cannot be learned from text. Drafted se203-v01 for "Plan anchors, concessions, and trades for a pricing discussion before it starts".
- **Combined discovery practice.** Lessons 2–4 each practice in isolation; there is no rehearsal that chains contract → funnel → objection diagnosis before the capstone. Drafted se203-x01 for "Apply the Sandler method's rapport-building, up-front contract, and pain funnel in a live conversation" and "Diagnose an objection before answering it, and choose a response that keeps the deal on merit".
- **Insight library.** Lesson 5 asks learners to harvest insights but gives only one fully built example per product. A bank of three scored insights (one per running example) would help "Deliver a commercial insight that reframes how the buyer sees their own situation".
- **Mutual action plan templates.** Lesson 8's worked plan is excellent; a blank template with column guidance would speed practice for "Close on a mutual action plan with named owners and dates".
- **Ramp arithmetic.** Lesson 7 Practice 2 asks for a ramp without a worked TCV comparison until now; the corrected Lesson 7 numbers plus se203-a01 address "Negotiate non-price contract terms without giving away margin".
