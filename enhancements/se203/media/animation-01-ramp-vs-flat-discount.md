## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: "the buyer wants a lower year-one number, so I have to cut the price." The animation compares, over three years plus the first renewal, a flat 20% discount on 400 seats with Lesson 7's ramp (300 / 400 / 450 seats at the full $500 rate). Both lower year one; only one keeps the rate intact and carries it into renewal.

## Visual language

- Two lanes, top "Flat 20% discount," bottom "Ramp at full rate." Each year is a column of seat blocks (each block = 50 seats) with a price tag.
- Okabe-Ito: flat discount blocks orange #E69F00 with diagonal hatch; ramp blocks blue #0072B2 solid; renewal baseline marker vermillion #D55E00 dashed line. Every lane, bar, and marker is text-labeled.
- Running TCV counter at the right of each lane.

## Interaction variant

Sliders for seats per year and per-seat rate; the learner tries to beat a target year-one number while keeping the rate at list. Counters show TCV and renewal baseline; a warning appears if the rate drops.
