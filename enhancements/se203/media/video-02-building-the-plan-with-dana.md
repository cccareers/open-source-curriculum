## Purpose

After watching, the learner can build a mutual action plan live with a buyer, working backwards from the buyer's go-live date, and surface the hidden step and the unnamed person who decide whether that date is possible.

## Audience and prerequisites

Learners who have read Lesson 8 through "Building it in the conversation." Same deal: support platform; Dana, Director of Support; target go-live 1 November; today is 14 July.

## Accessibility

- Captions with speaker labels.

- Every table row is read aloud as it appears; the full table is provided as an accessible download.

## Check for understanding

1. Why did Alex start from 1 November instead of today? *(Answer: working backwards shows which steps have to start now, especially the six-week security review that has to begin by 12 August.)*
2. Which question surfaced the security step? *(Answer: "What has to happen before a signature that I might not know about?")*
3. Why ask "whose signature is on it?" before the plan is agreed? *(Answer: it finds the real decider early enough to meet them; closing a champion who can't sign produces a yes with no deal.)*
