## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: "A resume is where I describe myself well." The animation shows a resume bullet as the top of a chain — bullet → portfolio caption card → metrics story → tracker row — and shows what a reviewer does when one link is missing. It also shows a vague claim being upgraded through three stages: vague, quantified, verifiable.

## Visual language (shapes, colors with color-blind-safe palette, labels)

- Four documents as stacked cards from top to bottom: Resume/LinkedIn, Portfolio (caption card), Metrics story, Tracker (spreadsheet rows).
- A "verification thread" is a solid line connecting a claim to its source; a missing link is a broken dashed line with a gap icon.
- Palette (Okabe-Ito): solid threads in bluish green #009E73 with check icons; broken threads in vermillion #D55E00 with a gap icon; documents in neutral gray with black text. Icons always accompany color.
- A reviewer is shown as a simple figure with a stopwatch.

## Scenes

| # | Duration | Frame description | Motion | Caption / VO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-12s | Text line: "Experienced with professional outreach and networking." Label "Vague." Then it rewrites to "Completed 122 outreach contacts over 8 weeks." Label "Quantified." Then extends: "…improving reply rate from 10% to 26% after rewriting the opening line (pipeline data and before/after messages in portfolio)." Label "Verifiable." | Text morphs in three steps; labels stamp in. | "A claim has three possible levels. Vague. Quantified. Verifiable — what you did, the number, and where the proof lives." |
| 2 | 12-30s | The verifiable bullet sits on the resume card. Reviewer with stopwatch taps "26%." A thread drops to the portfolio caption card ("Result: 10.3% → 25.9% reply rate"), then to the metrics story segment table (Weeks 1-3: 7/68; Weeks 4-6: 14/54), then to two highlighted tracker rows. Stopwatch stops at 0:42. | Thread draws downward card by card, checks appear at each link. | "A reviewer taps the number. Here's the trail: caption card, metrics story, tracker rows. Found in forty-two seconds. That claim holds." |
| 3 | 30-45s | A second bullet: "Booked 15 informational interviews." Reviewer taps. Thread drops to portfolio — no matching caption. Broken thread, gap icon. Stopwatch runs past 1:00 and flashes. | Thread breaks; stopwatch overruns. | "A second claim. No caption card, no tracker row. The stopwatch runs out. The reviewer doesn't conclude 'missing evidence.' They conclude 'inflated.'" |
| 4 | 45-58s | The bullet gets corrected from the tracker: "Booked and completed 9 informational interviews" — new thread connects cleanly. A LinkedIn card beside the resume shows the same number; a "=" sign appears between them. | Text corrects; thread reconnects; equality sign pops. | "Fix it from the source, and match it everywhere. Same number on the resume, LinkedIn, and application." |
| 5 | 58-70s | Summary: three rules appear: "Every claim → a proof location" · "Same number everywhere" · "No proof? Remove it, or go create the evidence." | Rules type in. | "Every claim points at proof. Every repeat matches. And if there's no proof, cut the claim — or go make the evidence." |

## Interaction variant (optional)

A "spot check" game: learners see a mock resume with six bullets and a mock portfolio and tracker. They click a bullet to trace it; a timer runs. Two bullets are seeded with broken links or mismatched numbers. Feedback shows what a reviewer would infer.

## Production notes
