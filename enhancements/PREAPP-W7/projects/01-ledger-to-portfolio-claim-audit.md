## Scenario

An apprenticeship reviewer has your application, your resume, your LinkedIn profile, and a link to your Outreach Portfolio. Before inviting you to interview, they pick five claims at random and try to verify each one in under a minute. Every claim that fails costs you more than a modest claim would have.

This project runs that review on yourself before a real reviewer does. You trace every claim across your four public documents back to a portfolio item, and every portfolio number back to your evidence ledger and CRM. Along the way, the ledger you have been building since Week 1 becomes the spine of the capstone.

## What you will produce

1. **Fit matrix sourced from the ledger**: each cell cites a ledger row, caption card, or dated reflection.
2. **Claim register**: one row per claim made in the application, resume, LinkedIn, and portfolio, each with its evidence location and verification status.
3. **Audit report** (half a page): claims checked, claims fixed, claims removed, and what you learned.
4. **Ledger update**: Week 7 row, final caption card index, dated reflection.

## Before you start (prerequisites, starter files or data)

- Evidence ledger, Weeks 1-6 (Weeks 1-6 supplementary projects). If yours has gaps, see the instructor notes; do not invent the missing weeks.
- Fit matrix and declaration memo in progress (Lesson 2).
- Outreach Portfolio draft (Lesson 3).
- Application draft, updated resume, and LinkedIn changes (Lesson 4) — or at least drafts.

Claim register columns:

```text
# | claim text (exact) | appears in (app / resume / LinkedIn / portfolio)
| evidence location (portfolio section + ledger row + CRM/repo/analytics source)
| verified by (me / partner) | status (OK / fixed / removed) | note
```

## Milestones

1. **Source the fit matrix** (45 min). For each of the four pathways, fill the performance and reflections columns from the ledger: the week's numbers row and the dated reflection for that rotation. Quote reflections exactly and date them. Where a reflection is missing, write "no contemporaneous reflection" and use labeled notes instead (Lesson 2).
2. **Build the metrics story from the ledger** (45 min). Use the weekly rows to segment the program (for example, Weeks 1-3 vs. 4-6) and find the turn. Every figure in the story must cite a ledger row. If you built the Week 5 funnel script, run it on your current CRM export and use its output.
3. **Index the artifacts** (30 min). List every caption card in the ledger. Choose at least one per rotation (W3 call review/funnel brief, W4 runbook/audit, W5 funnel script repo, W6 campaign tracker/results card) plus your strongest two or three overall. Check each caption card has skill, context, result, and your part.
4. **Build the claim register** (45 min). Copy every factual claim from your application, resume, LinkedIn headline/About/Experience, and portfolio into the register — exact text. For each, record where the evidence lives.
5. **Consistency pass** (20 min). Sort the register by number. Any number that appears in more than one document must be identical everywhere (Lesson 4: if they differ, a reviewer notices).
6. **Partner verification** (30 min). Your partner picks five claims at random and times you finding each source. Anything over a minute, or not found, is fixed or removed — before you submit.
7. **Audit report and ledger** (25 min). Write the half-page report. Add the Week 7 row and a dated reflection: what the evidence said about your pathway, and where it disagreed with your enthusiasm.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every fit-matrix cell cites a ledger entry, a dated reflection, interview notes, or coach input — or says "no evidence."
- [ ] Every number in the metrics story traces to a ledger row and an underlying system (CRM, repo, analytics).
- [ ] At least one artifact per rotation, each with a complete caption card.
- [ ] The claim register covers all four documents, with no claim missing an evidence location.
- [ ] Every repeated number is identical across documents.
- [ ] A partner verified five random claims, each found in under a minute (after fixes).
- [ ] Audit report lists claims fixed and removed, with counts.

## Evidence checklist

- Fit matrix with citations
- Claim register (spreadsheet)
- Partner's five-claim verification log with times
- Audit report
- Final ledger

## Rubric

| Criterion | Developing | Meets | Exceeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence sourcing | Matrix and story rely on memory | Every cell and figure cites ledger or system | Gaps are labeled honestly and one was filled with new, real evidence this week |
| Consistency | Numbers differ across documents | All repeated numbers identical | Register also standardizes wording of key claims (e.g. "reply rate" always means the same calculation) |
| Verification | Partner could not find several sources | Five random claims verified under a minute | Learner removed at least one claim they liked because the evidence was weak |
| Portfolio coverage | Missing rotations or caption fields | All rotations covered, complete caption cards | Ordering makes the declared pathway obvious without hiding the others |
| Reflection | Restates the decision | Names where evidence and enthusiasm agreed or disagreed | Names what evidence would change the decision (matches memo section 4) |

## Stretch goals

- Turn the claim register into a "proof index" page inside the portfolio: each headline claim links straight to its evidence.
- Ask your coach to run a two-claim spot check without warning, as a final test.

## Reflection prompts

- Which claim were you most reluctant to remove? Why did you want to keep it?
- Looking at your Week 1 reflection beside your Week 6 reflection, what changed in what you found boring?

## Instructor notes (common pitfalls, how to adapt for time)

- **Learners without a ledger** (skipped earlier supplementary projects): they build the claim register directly from CRM, repository, analytics, and lab notes. It takes longer but works. Do not let them backfill a "ledger" with remembered numbers labeled as weekly records.
- **Pitfall: rounding up.** 25.9% becomes "about 30%" somewhere. The consistency pass catches it.
- **Pitfall: names of real contacts in the portfolio.** Redact to roles unless the person gave permission.
- **Short on time:** Milestones 4-6 (register, consistency, partner verification) are the core and take about 1.6 hours; they directly protect the application.
- **Arc:** seventh link (`enhancements/PREAPP-ARC.md`). The verified claim register is the source list for Week 8's evidence slides.
