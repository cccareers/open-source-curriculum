## Summary

Week 7 is rigorous and well framed: "enthusiasm is a signal, not a decision" and "the application is a citation exercise" are exactly right for career-changers. The structural problem sits upstream. The week depends on evidence (end-of-rotation reflections, weekly numbers, before/after artifacts) that Weeks 1-6 never explicitly tell learners to capture. This pass labels reconstructed evidence honestly in-lesson and adds a supplementary evidence ledger arc across Weeks 1-7 (see `enhancements/PREAPP-ARC.md`).

## Depth and coverage gaps

- **No example declaration memo** for "Evaluate pathway fit with evidence and justify the choice in writing." The five sections are clear; one fully written memo (the drafted video provides a partial one for "Keisha") would set the bar.
- **No example Personal Prospecting Playbook** for "Assemble a Personal Prospecting Playbook and best artifacts from each rotation." A one-page model with the six sections would help, especially "What failed."
- **No guidance on privacy in the portfolio.** Artifacts from Weeks 2-6 contain real contacts' names and messages. The lessons should tell learners to redact to roles unless they have permission.
- **The claim-verification drill (W7-04 Practice 5) checks only three numbers.** The supplementary project expands this into a full claim register.

## Assessment ideas

- **Claim ladder:** ten claims; learners label vague/quantified/verifiable and upgrade three.
- **Matrix critique:** a matrix with adjectives and empty cells; learners fix it.
- **Caption card completeness check:** five cards, find the missing or weak fields.
