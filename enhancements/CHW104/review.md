## Summary
CHW104 handles high-stakes content carefully: scope is restated in every lesson, language is person-first and weight-inclusive, and trauma-informed practice is framed as universal rather than a specialty. The biggest opportunity is integration — the course has no running client, so learners never see trust-building, medication support, nutrition, and trauma-informed practice applied to the same person. Project x01 supplies that case. A few clinical-adjacent statements needed precision (asthma inhaler types, trauma-informed framework attribution).

## Depth and coverage gaps
- **No running case.** Project x01 (Mr. Ellis) integrates all four lessons. Objective: "Support clients with chronic disease basics, medication adherence, nutrition, and weight-inclusive care within CHW scope."
- **Older adults section is brief.** Add one worked scenario on speaking to the older adult rather than the family member, and on medication lists across multiple prescribers. Objective: "Adapt services for formerly incarcerated individuals, older adults, and communities experiencing trauma."
- **Community-level trauma** section would benefit from a California-relevant example the owner selects (e.g., a community's experience with immigration enforcement near clinics) handled with care. Objective: "Apply trust-building and culturally responsive approaches with high-risk and underserved populations."
- **Secondary traumatic stress** gets one section; a short self-check tool (not a clinical instrument) and supervision prompt would deepen it. Objective: "Apply trauma-informed principles across CHW services."
- **Materials selection** would benefit from a worked before/after handout example. Objective: "Choose education materials appropriate to the audience's culture and literacy."
- **Check-your-understanding** added to Lesson 3 in this pass.

## Open questions for the course owner
- **Clinical review:** the plain-language condition explanations, emergency signs, BP technique, and inhaler content should be reviewed by a licensed clinician before publication, and re-reviewed when guidelines change (asthma guidance in particular has shifted toward combination inhaler regimens for some patients).
- **Reentry Medi-Cal:** California's pre-release Medi-Cal services and enrollment for people leaving incarceration are being phased in; confirm current status before teaching "no insurance active on day one."
- **Program protocol for vitals:** confirm whether CHWs in your placements take readings themselves under protocol, or only teach self-monitoring; Lesson 3's practice assumes teaching.
- Does the program want an explicit secondary traumatic stress resource list (EAP, peer support) in Lesson 5?
