## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: "If a contact is in my CRM, I'm working them." Reality: a contact without a dated next action never appears in the daily view, so it silently dies. The animation also shows *why* the daily view is ordered replies first, follow-ups second, new sends last: value follows the relationship already built.

## Visual language (shapes, colors with color-blind-safe palette, labels)

- Contacts are small circles with initials. Tier 1 (employer partner) circles carry a star badge, not just a different color.
- Stages are labeled columns left to right: Researched, Initiated, Connected, In conversation, Interviewed. Nurture and Closed sit below as labeled trays.
- A small calendar-tag icon attached to a circle = "has a dated next action."
- Palette (Okabe-Ito): blue #0072B2 for contacts with a next action, orange #E69F00 for contacts without one, vermillion #D55E00 for "lost," gray #999999 for background. Every state also has a shape/icon cue: next action = calendar tag; no next action = no tag plus a dashed outline; lost = circle fades and drops through the floor.

## Interaction variant (optional)

Step-through H5P or a simple web interactive: the learner gets 15 contacts and a day counter. They click a contact to "log a touch" and must choose whether to set a next-action date. Advancing the day shows which contacts surface in Today and which fade. End screen reports "contacts lost to no next action."
