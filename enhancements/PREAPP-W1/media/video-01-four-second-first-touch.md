## Purpose

After watching, a learner can take a generic connection request and rewrite it into the four-part structure (hook, bridge, ask, signature), test the hook with the paste test, and fit it under the connection-note character limit.

## Audience and prerequisites

Week 1 participants on day one or two. They have a target list in their CRM and have not yet sent many messages. No sales background assumed.

## Script

| Time | Visual / On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Talking head, instructor at desk. Lower third: "Week 1 — Outreach Fundamentals." | "Somebody you've never met is about to give your message four seconds. In this video we'll write a message that survives those four seconds — and we'll do it by fixing a bad one." |
| 0:15 | Cut to screen: a LinkedIn-style message box (mock UI, no real brand). Text types in: "Hi, I'm transitioning into tech and would love to connect and learn about any opportunities at your company. Thanks!" | "Here's the message almost everyone sends first. Let's read it from the other side, the way a busy hiring manager does." |
| 0:30 | Two questions appear beside the message: "Does this person know who I am?" "What will this cost me?" | "In those four seconds the reader is answering two questions. Does this person know who I am? And what is this going to cost me? This message fails both. Nothing in it is about them, and 'any opportunities' asks them to do your job search for you." |
| 0:55 | Message box clears. Four labeled empty slots appear stacked: HOOK, BRIDGE, ASK, SIGNATURE. | "So we rebuild it in four parts. Hook, bridge, ask, signature. In that order." |
| 1:05 | Split screen: left, Dana's LinkedIn post (mock, fictional person): "We moved our whole support team onto a new ticketing system this quarter. The hard part wasn't the software — it was training staff who'd never used one." Right, the HOOK slot. | "The hook is proof you did the work. I spent five minutes on Dana's profile and found this post. That's my material." |
| 1:20 | HOOK slot fills: "Your post on moving support onto the new ticketing stack, especially training non-technical staff, stuck with me." | "Here's the hook. Now the test. I'm going to paste this sentence into a message to someone else." |
| 1:35 | The hook sentence is dragged into a second message box addressed to "Hi Luis —". A red outline and the label "Doesn't make sense for Luis" appear. | "Would it make sense sent to Luis, who works in finance? No. That's what we want. If a sentence survives being pasted to a different person, it is not a hook — it's filler." |
| 1:55 | Show a failing hook for contrast: "I saw your company is growing." Paste it into Luis's box; label "Still makes sense — not a hook." | "Compare: 'I saw your company is growing.' That works for Luis, for Dana, for everyone. Which means it proves nothing." |
| 2:15 | BRIDGE slot fills: "I'm aiming at support engineering," | "The bridge connects their world to yours in one clause. Not your life story. Just: here's why their detail matters to me." |
| 2:30 | ASK slot fills: "I'd value 15 min on what you look for in someone making that jump." | "The ask is small and specific. Fifteen minutes, one topic. Not 'any opportunities.' A specific ask is easy to say yes to; a vague one is easy to ignore." |
| 2:50 | SIGNATURE slot fills: "I'm a CCC tech pre-apprentice" … "— Marcus" | "The signature says who you are in one clause. 'CCC tech pre-apprentice' tells them you're in a structured training program — and employer partners recognize it." |
| 3:05 | All four slots merge into one message. A character counter in the corner reads 167 / 200. | "Put together, it looks like this. And notice the counter. LinkedIn connection notes have a character limit — 200 according to current LinkedIn Help, with a monthly cap on personalized notes for basic accounts, so check yours. | 3:25 | Show the 347-character version morphing into the 167-character version; deleted words fade out in gray with strikethrough, kept words stay black. | "To trim, cut words, not parts. The hook names the training detail, the bridge names support, the ask becomes a question about the path, and the signature keeps the program and name. All four parts are still there." |
| 3:50 | Final message on screen: "Hi Dana — your post on training staff on the new ticketing system stuck with me. I'm a CCC tech pre-apprentice aiming at support; could I ask about your path? — Marcus" | "Read it out loud. Does it sound like a person? Good. That's your first touch." |
| 4:05 | Cut to CRM mock: contact record for Dana. Stage changes from Researched to Initiated; activity logged "Connection request sent"; next action "Touch 2" dated three days out. | "And you're not done when you hit send. Log it right now, inside the block. Stage to Initiated. Activity recorded. Next action dated three days out — that's touch two." |
| 4:30 | Timeline graphic: Day 0, Day 3, Day 8, Day 15, each with a one-line description. | "If Dana doesn't reply, that's normal. Touch two on day three adds something new. ## On-screen assets and B-roll

- Verify note and monthly invitation limits against [LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563153?lang=en) before recording. If the account is capped, show professional email as an available outreach route; do not imply fifty personalized notes are available on a basic account.
- Mock LinkedIn-style message UI with a live character counter (do not use real LinkedIn branding or real people's profiles).
- Fictional profile post for "Dana" (support operations lead at a fictional regional company).
- Mock CRM contact record with stage dropdown, activity log, and next-action date field.
- Follow-up timeline graphic (Day 0 / 3 / 8 / 15).

## Accessibility

- Burned-in or sidecar captions for all narration; on-screen text read aloud in full when it first appears.
- Pass/fail on the paste test is shown with both color (red/green) **and** a text label and icon (cross / check), never color alone.
- Strikethrough plus gray for deleted words, so the trim edit is visible without color perception.
- Mock UI typed at readable speed (no faster than 15 characters per second) and held on screen at least 3 seconds after completion.
- Provide a transcript with the final message text as plain text for screen-reader users.

## Check for understanding

1. *"Hi Jordan — I saw your company is hiring and wanted to connect."* Does this have a hook? **Answer:** No. Paste it to any other contact at any hiring company and it still makes sense, so it proves no research.
2. Your best draft is 340 characters and the limit is 300. What do you cut? **Answer:** Words inside each part (shorter phrasing), not whole parts. All four — hook, bridge, ask, signature — must survive.
3. You sent a first touch and logged it. What else must be on the record before you close it? **Answer:** A next action with a date (touch 2, three days out). A contact with no dated next action drops out of your daily view.
