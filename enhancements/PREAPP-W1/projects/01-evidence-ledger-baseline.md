## Scenario

In Week 7 you will build the Outreach Portfolio: a metrics story, a Personal Prospecting Playbook, and your best artifacts from every week, each one backed by numbers a recruiter can check. Participants who wait until Week 7 to collect that evidence spend the week reconstructing it from memory, and memory inflates the good weeks and loses the bad ones.

This project starts the fix on day five of the program. You will open an **evidence ledger** — one spreadsheet (or one document with a table) that you add a few rows to every Friday for eight weeks. Every later supplementary project in this program adds to the same ledger, and the Week 7 capstone is assembled from it. Today's job is to record your honest Week 1 baseline: the numbers before you got good at this.

## What you will build / produce

One file, named `evidence-ledger` and stored in a personal cloud account you will still own after the program ends. It has three tabs (or three headed sections):

1. **Weekly numbers** — one row per week, starting with Week 1.
2. **Artifacts** — one caption card per artifact (format below), starting with two from Week 1.
3. **Reflections** — one dated paragraph per week, starting with this one.

## Before you start (prerequisites, starter files or data)

- Your CRM is live, with your pre-work target list imported and tiered (Lesson 2, Practice 1-4).
- You have sent at least some first touches and logged them (Lesson 3).
- Your headline and About section are rewritten and your first post is live, or scheduled for this week (Lesson 4).
- Starter column headers for the Weekly numbers tab (copy these exactly — later weeks add columns, never rename these):

```text
week | dates | initiated_total | initiated_this_week | connected | in_conversation
| interviewed | connect_rate | conversation_rate | no_next_action | overdue_followups
| posts_published | notes
```

## Milestones

1. **Create the ledger** (15 min). Make the file and the three tabs in a personal account. Paste the column headers above. Share view access with your instructor.
2. **Record the Week 1 numbers** (30 min). Read each number off your CRM, not from memory. For `connected`, `in_conversation`, and `interviewed`, count every contact who has *reached at least* that stage — a contact now in "In conversation" also counts as connected. Compute `connect_rate` (connected ÷ initiated_total) and `conversation_rate` (in_conversation ÷ connected) to one decimal place. In `notes`, write which number you most want to move in Week 2.
3. **Caption your first artifact: the before-and-after first touch** (30 min). Take the first message you wrote this week and your best message from Friday. Paste both into the Artifacts tab with a caption card:

```text
Artifact:  First touch, Monday vs. Friday (Week 1)
Skill:     Writing a personalized connection request with a checkable hook
Context:   Cold LinkedIn outreach to regional employers, 300-character note limit
Result:    [e.g. Monday version: 0 of 8 accepted; Friday version: 3 of 9 accepted]
My part:   Wrote both; Friday hook revised after instructor feedback in lab
```

4. **Caption your second artifact: the alignment check** (30 min). Screenshot your headline before and after (or paste the text), plus the sent message you used for the alignment check in Lesson 4. Caption it the same way. In `Result`, write what changed and any movement in profile views or acceptances you noticed over 48 hours — "no measurable change yet" is an acceptable, honest result.
5. **Write your Week 1 reflection** (20 min). Date it. Answer three questions in one paragraph each: Which part of the week was hard? Which part was *boring*? What surprised you about how people responded? (Week 7 asks you to weigh reflections written at the time — this is the first one.)
6. **Verification pass** (15 min). Swap ledgers with a partner. They pick any two numbers and ask you to show the source in the CRM. Fix anything you cannot show within a minute.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The ledger lives in a personal account (not a cohort or program account) and your instructor can view it.
- [ ] The Week 1 row is complete, with both rates computed and a "number to move" note.
- [ ] Every count matches what your CRM shows today; a partner verified two of them.
- [ ] Two caption cards exist, each with all five fields filled.
- [ ] At least one result field reports something that did not work or did not move.
- [ ] A dated Week 1 reflection answers all three questions.

## Instructor notes (common pitfalls, how to adapt for time)

- **Pitfall: current-stage counts.** Most CRMs report how many contacts are *in* a stage right now. A contact who replied and then booked a call leaves "Connected," which makes connect rate look worse as the learner gets better. Insist on "reached at least this stage" counts from day one; it saves a confusing conversation in Week 3.
- **Pitfall: storing in a program account.** Week 8 requires participants to prove they own their data. Starting the ledger in a personal account avoids a migration later.
- **Short on time:** Milestones 1, 2, and 5 are the core (about 65 minutes). Caption cards can be done in Week 2's Friday block.
- **This is the first link in an eight-week chain.** See `enhancements/PREAPP-ARC.md`. Each later supplementary project adds one row, one or two caption cards, and one reflection to this same file.
