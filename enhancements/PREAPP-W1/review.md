## Summary

These are strong lab guides: direct, concrete, and every section ends in action on a real record. The biggest opportunities are (1) defining jargon for career-changers who meet "CRM," "InMail," and "quota" here for the first time, (2) a funnel-counting ambiguity that makes rates fall as learners improve, and (3) starting the evidence trail the Week 7 capstone depends on, which this pass adds as a supplementary "evidence ledger" project.

## Depth and coverage gaps

- **Funnel reading has no worked example** (objective: "Stand up a live prospecting pipeline in a CRM with the target list staged and prioritized"). Added a 40-contact worked calculation in W1-02.
- **No worked example of a follow-up sequence** (objective: "Write personalized connection requests and follow-ups and run a daily outreach routine at quota"). Lesson 3 describes touches 2-4 but shows only touch 1. Recommend adding the full four-touch sequence for Dana as a code block in a future pass (the Practice item 3 asks learners to produce exactly this without a model).
- **Import mechanics are vendor-neutral but thin.** Learners who have never used a spreadsheet may struggle with "standardize company names." A short instructor handout (find-and-replace, TRIM, sort-to-find-duplicates) would help; keep it out of the lesson to preserve vendor neutrality.
- **No explicit "check your understanding" blocks.** Added one to W1-04; W1-02 and W1-03 are served by the new video and animation checks.
- **Evidence capture for Week 7 starts too late.** Weeks 1-6 never instruct learners to save dated metrics, before/after messages, or reflections, yet W7-02 and W7-03 require all three. Addressed by the supplementary project and the program-wide arc (`enhancements/PREAPP-ARC.md`).

## Proposed additional projects

- **Drafted:** `projects/01-evidence-ledger-baseline.md` — start the evidence ledger that every later week adds to; record honest Week 1 baseline numbers, two caption cards, and the first dated reflection.
- Idea: "Dud Rescue Clinic" — in pairs, each learner brings three no-response first touches; partner rewrites the hook and ask; both versions are sent to comparable contacts the next day and results logged.
- Idea: "Import Cleanup Drill" — instructor provides a deliberately messy 60-row target list (three spellings of one company, split names, blank titles); learners clean and import into a sandbox CRM against a timed checklist.

## Assessment ideas

- **Hook or not?** Ten one-line openers; learners mark each as hook/not-hook and justify with the paste test.
- **Funnel calculation item:** given a CRM stage table (current-stage counts), compute "reached at least" counts and both rates.
- **Alignment audit rubric:** four rows (role direction, capabilities, program affiliation, tone), scored match/mismatch, applied by a peer to a message + profile pair.
