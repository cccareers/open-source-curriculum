## Purpose

After watching, the learner can open a renewal conversation from evidence: recap the promise, present the misses first, ask "how are you thinking about next year?" and wait, then answer a seat-reduction request by working through utilization region by region instead of arguing.

## Audience and prerequisites

Learners who have read Lesson 4. Tallgrass Facilities Group, as at the end of Lesson 5's update: renewal 5 January; 400 seats at $23.75 per seat per month; late-billed call-outs down from 12.4% to 4.1%; West recovered from 11% to 74% after the offline logout fix; Marcus Bell (CFO) has seen results twice. One of four measures is still behind. Dana Whitfield is the champion.

## Check for understanding

1. Why does Alex present the missed measure first? *(Answer: conceding the miss up front makes the rest of the evidence believable and frames the meeting as a working partnership.)*
2. What question does Alex ask before going silent? *(Answer: "Given all that, how are you thinking about next year?")*
3. How does Alex handle "we'd want to come down on seats"? *(Answer: he agrees to look at it together, goes region by region with Dana's hiring plans, and offers two modelled options with a date. No argument and no discount.)*
