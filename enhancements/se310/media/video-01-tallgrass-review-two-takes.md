## Purpose

After watching, the learner can hear a hedge, repeat it, and wait; turn "it's fine" into a specific mechanism; and leave the meeting with the one fact that could end the account — then write it down in the Heard / Concluded / Committed format.

## Audience and prerequisites

Learners who have read Lesson 3. Dramatizes Lesson 3's worked review (Tallgrass Facilities Group, month seven, Dana Whitfield) and its "how the same conversation goes wrong" contrast, so learners can see and hear the moments the text annotates.

## Script

Setting: video call. **Rep** and **Dana Whitfield**, Director of Field Operations. A small on-screen "listening for" card in the corner reads: *Unmet expectation · Effort · Change · Comparison*. When one appears, the matching word lights with an icon.

| Time | Visual / On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Host to camera. | "Month seven. On paper Tallgrass is healthy: late-billed call-outs are down from 12.4% to 4.1%. Two reps run the same forty-minute review with the same customer. One leaves with a pleasant feeling. The other leaves with the fact that decides the renewal." |
| 0:20 | Title: "Take 1." | |
| 0:22 | Rep (Jordan). | **Jordan:** "Hi Dana, how's everything going with the platform?" |
| 0:25 | Dana. | **Dana:** "Yeah, good. It's been a big improvement." |
| 0:28 | Jordan reads from a slide. | **Jordan:** "Great to hear. So late billing's down from 12.4 to 4.1 percent, which is ahead of plan, and adoption's at 68 percent across the regions..." |
| 0:38 | Dana. | **Dana:** "That's great." |
| 0:40 | Jordan. | **Jordan:** "Any issues at all?" |
| 0:42 | Dana. | **Dana:** "Nothing major." |
| 0:44 | Jordan. | **Jordan:** "Perfect. I'll send this over. Same time next quarter?" |
| 0:48 | Freeze. Callouts with icons and text. | Host VO: "Pleasant, efficient, and empty. A verdict question invited a polite verdict. Sixty-eight percent adoption was read aloud and nobody asked which region was missing. 'Any issues at all?' was asked in a tone that requests 'no.' The account is five months from renewal and there is no evidence for it." |
| 1:25 | Title: "Take 2." | |
| 1:28 | Rep (Alex). Card: no word lit yet. | **Alex:** "Before I go through the numbers — walk me through what a bad week looks like for dispatch now. Not an average week, a bad one." |
| 1:36 | Dana. Chip under her line: "hedge." | **Dana:** "Honestly it's much better. The bad weeks are mostly billing now rather than dispatch." |
| 1:42 | Alex. | **Alex:** "Mostly billing." |
| 1:44 | Silence; caption "[3-second pause]". | |
| 1:47 | Dana. Card lights "Effort." | **Dana:** "...Yeah. Well — West is still on paper, so their call-outs come to us as a spreadsheet on Monday and someone here keys them in. That's about four hours of Ruth's week." |
| 1:58 | Alex. | **Alex:** "So West going live at day 85 — that didn't hold." |
| 2:02 | Dana. Card lights "Unmet expectation." | **Dana:** "It went live technically. The crews just aren't using it. Ray runs West and he's never been convinced." |
| 2:08 | Silence; caption "[5-second pause]". | |
| 2:13 | Dana. | **Dana:** "To be fair, he's got a point about the app in the yard. It logs them out and they lose the form. So they gave up." |
| 2:20 | Alex. | **Alex:** "That's the first I've heard of the logout. Tell me exactly what happens — a technician's in the yard, they open the form, and then?" |
| 2:27 | Dana. | **Dana:** "They fill it in, signal drops, and when it comes back they're at the login screen and the form's gone. Twice a day, Ray says." |
| 2:34 | Alex. | **Alex:** "What else has that changed? Does anything downstream depend on West's data being a week behind?" |
| 2:40 | Dana. Card lights "Change." | **Dana:** "Marcus's month-end. He asked me about it in April, actually. He wanted to know why we're paying for four hundred seats when three regions are on it." |
| 2:50 | Alex writes; does not respond for two seconds. | **Alex:** "Say more about that conversation." |
| 2:55 | Dana. | **Dana:** "It wasn't a big deal. He's doing a review of all the software spend before the budget goes in — September, I think. He asked me to have a number for what we're actually using." |

| 3:12 | Dana. | **Dana:** "That's about the size of it, yes." |
| 3:15 | Alex. | **Alex:** "Then I'd rather work on West than anything else I brought today. Is there anything else on your side I should know about before I take this back?" |
| 3:22 | Silence; caption "[5-second pause]". | |
| 3:27 | Dana. Card lights "Comparison." | **Dana:** "...Ray's been talking to someone about a different scheduling app. I don't know how serious it is." |
| 3:33 | Freeze. All four words lit. | Host VO: "All four categories — effort, unmet expectation, change, comparison — arrived. None was volunteered. They came from a question about a bad week, one repeated word, two silences, and 'say more' at the moment Marcus's name appeared." |
| 4:10 | Note on screen, typed out in three labelled blocks. | Host VO: "And the meeting isn't over until it's written down the same day — Heard, Concluded, Committed." On screen: **Heard:** "West is still on paper" — crews abandoned the app because it logs out when signal drops (twice a day, per Ray). Ruth keys a West spreadsheet ~4 hrs/week. Marcus asked in April for a utilization number ahead of a September software spend review. Ray "talking to someone about a different scheduling app." **Concluded (mine):** West non-adoption is an offline session-handling defect, not training. True-down risk 400 → ~270 seats. The September spend review is the real decision point. **Committed:** I raise the logout with delivery by 16 Aug; date to Dana by 21 Aug. Dana sets up a site visit with Ray. I ask Dana to introduce me to Marcus before September. |

| 5:55 | Recap card: ask about their world; ask for the exception; repeat hedges; stay silent; say more when a name or a date appears; play back the risk; write Heard / Concluded / Committed. | |
| 6:15 | End card. | |

## On-screen assets and B-roll

- Video-call frames; corner "listening for" card with four words that light with an icon plus bold text.
- Typed note animation in three labelled blocks.

## Accessibility

- Captions with speaker labels; pauses captioned with duration.
- The "listening for" card changes use icon + bold + underline, not color alone, and the host names each category aloud.
- Full transcript and the note text provided.

## Check for understanding

1. Which single word from Dana opened the conversation, and what did Alex do with it? *(Answer: "mostly" — he repeated "Mostly billing" and stopped talking.)*
2. Why is "Say more about that conversation" the most important line in the meeting? *(Answer: it followed the first mention of the economic buyer and a spend review — the change that actually decides the renewal — instead of reassuring Dana.)*
3. Put "West non-adoption is a product-fit problem" in the right part of the note. *(Answer: Concluded — it is the rep's interpretation, kept separate from what Dana said.)*
