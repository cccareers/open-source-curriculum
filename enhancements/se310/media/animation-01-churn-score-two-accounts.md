## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: high usage and no complaints mean a safe renewal. The animation builds Lesson 4's weighted scorecard for two accounts side by side, factor by factor, so the learner watches Harbourline (91% utilization, nobody complaining) overtake Tallgrass (visible West problem) — 27 vs. 26 — because its relationship and value factors are empty.

## Visual language

- Two vertical "risk thermometers" labelled Tallgrass and Harbourline, scale 0–60 with band markers: green 0–7, amber 8–15, red 16–24, critical 25+. Bands also labelled with text and distinct hatch patterns.
- Each factor is a block that drops into the thermometer; block height = weighted score; block label shows factor name and "score × weight."
- Four factor groups shown by icon: Adoption (bar chart icon), Relationship (two-person icon), Value (scale icon), Commercial (briefcase icon).
