## Summary
CHW101 is strong: the lessons are concrete, scope-aware, person-first, and consistently tie concepts to what a CHW says and does. The biggest opportunity is a recurring fictional client (the course currently uses one-off vignettes) so learners can carry one case from SDOH identification through navigation to pattern reporting, plus several time-sensitive Medi-Cal and marketplace statements that need a current-policy check before each cohort.

## Depth and coverage gaps
- **No running client.** Each lesson invents a new vignette. A single fictional client (see project x01, client H.R.) would let learners see SDOH → navigation → pattern → outcomes as one story. Objective: "Explain health equity and identify social determinants of health affecting clients and communities."
- **Coverage basics lack a worked conversation.** The coverage section is accurate but expository; a short kitchen-table dialogue explaining a Medi-Cal renewal notice would make "at a client-support level" concrete. Objective: "Explain coverage basics (Medicaid/Medi-Cal, Medicare, marketplace plans) at a client-support level."
- **No answer keys.** CHW101-02 Practice 2 (scope sort) and CHW101-06 Practice 1 (twenty-item classification) have no instructor key. Ambiguous items (e.g., "89 percent of surveyed clients said they felt respected") should have a stated answer (short-term/experience-of-care outcome) and rationale. Objective: "Distinguish program outputs from outcomes in CHW impact studies."
- **Equity statistics are qualitative** ("a decade or more" life expectancy gap). That is appropriately hedged; if a number is added later, cite a California-specific source.
- **Policy-to-person exercise** (CHW101-05 Practice 5) would benefit from one worked example so learners see the expected length and specificity. Objective: "Connect policy-level decisions to the barriers clients experience locally."
- **Check-your-understanding blocks** were missing; added to CHW101-04 and CHW101-06 in this pass.

## Open questions for the course owner
- **Medi-Cal eligibility for undocumented adults (CHW101-04):** confirm current enrollment status, any enrollment freeze, and any premium or cost-sharing changes before each cohort; the lesson's present-tense sentence may be outdated.
- **Medi-Cal CHW benefit (CHW101-02):** the lesson says the benefit was added in 2022. Confirm the effective date and current supervision, recommendation, and qualification requirements with current DHCS guidance; the lesson correctly defers specifics, but instructors should have a current source on hand.
- **Federal Medicaid changes:** federal legislation enacted in 2025 adds renewal-frequency and work/community-engagement provisions for some expansion adults on a phased timeline. The lesson's "members must renew periodically" is still true, but instructors should verify what applies to California and when.
- **Marketplace subsidies (CHW101-04, CHW101-05):** the size of premium subsidies has changed when temporary enhancements expired; the lesson does not quote amounts (good), but practice items about "subsidy amount changed" may need current context.
- **Interpreter rights (CHW101-04):** "in most health settings receiving federal funds" is accurate in general (Title VI / Section 1557); confirm whether the program wants to also cite California-specific language access requirements for Medi-Cal plans.
- Should CHW101 adopt a single recurring fictional client across lessons (e.g., H.R. from project x01)? That would be a body edit across several lessons and was not made in this pass.
