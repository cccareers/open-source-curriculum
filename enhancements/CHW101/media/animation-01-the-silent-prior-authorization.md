## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: "If a client misses imaging or a specialist visit, they dropped the ball." Reality: the system is fragmented, and a prior authorization request can stall between the clinic and the plan with no one telling the client. This animation follows the fictional client from Lesson 4 (the woman who finds a lump) through each handoff and shows where a CHW's navigation steps — verify coverage, call together, log the reference number, follow up — keep the path from disappearing.

## Visual language

- The client is a single warm-orange circle labeled "Ms. Tran (fictional)." Organizations are labeled rounded rectangles: "Clinic," "Health plan," "Imaging center," "Mailbox."
- The path is a solid line; stalled steps turn into a dashed line with a pause icon and the word "STALLED."
- CHW actions appear as blue tags with a checkmark icon and text. Palette: Okabe-Ito (orange #E69F00, blue #0072B2, gray #999999, vermilion #D55E00 for "stalled"). Every status also has an icon and a word, so nothing depends on color.

## Interaction variant

A step-through H5P "Branching Scenario": at each handoff the learner chooses the CHW action (e.g., "Wait for the clinic to call" vs. "Call the plan with Ms. Tran and get a reference number"). Wrong choices show the path stalling; right choices advance it. End screen lists every navigation step from Lesson 4's ten-step sequence that the learner used.

## Production notes

- Keep insurance vocabulary on screen identical to Lesson 4's glossary (prior authorization, explanation of benefits, network).
- Do not show real plan logos or real letter templates. Fictional client name only.
- VO in English with a Spanish and Vietnamese caption track recommended for the CHW audience.
- Prior authorization processes vary by plan; the animation shows a generic pattern and should not claim specific timelines.
