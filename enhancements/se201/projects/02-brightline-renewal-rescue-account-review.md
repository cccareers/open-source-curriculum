## Scenario

This extends the Lesson 6 practice account. You have inherited **Brightline Manufacturing**, eight months into a twelve-month term: $148,000 ARR on 240 seats at $51.39 per seat per month; term ends 31 March; 45-day notice; auto-renews. Active seats have fallen from 218 to 176. Your champion, the Director of Operations, has been promoted to VP, with a new director, **Sam Patel**, underneath her. You have never met the CFO who signed. The IT Manager preferred the incumbent. Nobody recorded a baseline. A third division has asked for a demo. Your book is 18 accounts and $2.1M ARR, with about 5 hours a week for account management.

You get 30 minutes with the newly promoted VP, **Joanne Reyes**, and Sam. You will prepare a one-page account plan, run the meeting with a partner playing both roles, and leave with a dated plan both sides own.

## What you will produce

1. A one-page account plan in the six-part Lesson 6 format: snapshot, stakeholder map, value contract (rebuilt late), touchpoint calendar to renewal + 1 month, expansion path, and risk register.
2. A numbers sheet: Brightline's share of your book; ARR at risk if seats true down to active use; account-level NRR under three scenarios (true-down only; true-down plus the third division landing; flat renewal).
3. A recorded 30-minute account review with Joanne and Sam (partner plays both from the cards below).
4. A same-day follow-up email with the agreed value measures, owners, and dates.
5. A revised plan after the meeting.

## Before you start

Compute the numbers first:
- Share of book: $148,000 / $2,100,000 = 7.0%.
- True-down exposure: (240 − 176) × $51.39 × 12 = $39,468, so renewal ARR would be about $108,532.
- Notice deadline: 45 days before 31 March, about 14 February. Every renewal milestone is planned against this date, not 31 March.

**Persona cards (partner only)**

*Joanne Reyes, VP Operations (former champion)*
- Warm, but now responsible for five divisions. Will say "It's fine, Sam owns it now" unless asked what she'll be measured on this year.
- If asked: her new KPI is cost per unit across all divisions, and the CFO has asked every VP for a "software rationalization list" by 31 January.
- Will agree to introduce you to the CFO only if you offer something the CFO would find useful, such as a cost-per-unit before/after for the two live divisions.

*Sam Patel, Director of Operations*
- Inherited the platform and feels no ownership. Says "we use it" without enthusiasm.
- If asked where it works least well: the second-shift team at Plant 2 went back to paper after their supervisor left (about 40 seats).
- Can produce from Brightline's own MES export: scrap rate and changeover time by line. Will offer this only if asked what numbers he already reports upward.
- On the third division's demo request: "That's Mark's lot. Their budget's separate."

## Milestones

1. **Numbers sheet (45 min).**
2. **Draft plan (60 min).** Stakeholder map with single-threading risk named; three candidate value measures that can be sourced from Brightline's own systems; a touchpoint calendar that fits your time budget (show the arithmetic); the third division as a gated expansion path, not a proposal.
3. **Account review (30 min, recorded).** Goals: confirm two value measures with Sam as owner; uncover the rationalization list deadline; secure a CFO introduction; agree a response to Plant 2 second shift.
4. **Follow-up email (20 min).** Measures, baselines to be produced, owners, dates.
5. **Revise the plan (30 min).** Move the binding date if needed. The CFO's 31 January list may matter more than the 14 February notice date.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All three numbers computed with arithmetic shown, and NRR given for each scenario.
- [ ] Single-threading risk named, with a dated multithreading action (Sam, CFO, IT Manager).
- [ ] Two value measures agreed with Brightline's own system as the source and Sam as the owner.
- [ ] Plant 2 second shift is addressed with a named action, not a general "adoption push."
- [ ] The third division is held as a gated expansion with a trigger and review date, not proposed in this meeting.
- [ ] The touchpoint calendar fits within your share of 5 hours a week, with the arithmetic shown.
- [ ] Revised plan reflects the 31 January rationalization deadline.

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] Numbers sheet
- [ ] Draft and revised account plan
- [ ] Recording
- [ ] Follow-up email
- [ ] One-paragraph note on what changed between draft and revision

## Rubric

| Criterion | Developing | Meets | Exceeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Numbers | Errors or missing NRR scenarios | All correct, with arithmetic | Uses the numbers to tier Brightline and justify its share of your hours |
| Value contract | Vague measures or vendor-dashboard sources | Two measures from Brightline's systems with an owner | A defensible late baseline method, with its weakest part stated |
| Stakeholder coverage | Relies on Joanne | Plan reaches Sam, the CFO, and IT with dates | Ties the CFO introduction to Joanne's new KPI |
| Expansion discipline | Proposes the third division now | Gated with trigger and date | Explains with the utilization number why expansion now would damage the renewal |
| Meeting execution | Pitches or reports numbers | Asks about their world; surfaces the rationalization deadline | Leaves with dated commitments on both sides, confirmed in writing the same day |

## Reflection prompts

## Instructor notes

- Learners often pitch the third division to "offset" the true-down. That breaks the expand-from-proof rule in Lesson 6. Ask them for the utilization figure.
- The 31 January list only surfaces if someone asks Joanne what she is measured on. Replay the recording at that point if nobody does.
- For a shorter session, skip the recording and grade the plan and numbers sheet.
