## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: "the book grew 14%, so it is healthy." The animation builds Lesson 4's ARR bridge ($4.8M → $5.48M) as a waterfall, then strips new logos away to reveal the existing base shrank (NRR 96.25%). It closes with Rep A vs. Rep B reaching the same ending ARR at very different cost.

## Visual language

- Waterfall bars on a dollar axis labeled in $M.
- Okabe-Ito palette: beginning/ending ARR grey #999999; new logos blue #0072B2 with diagonal hatch; expansion bluish green #009E73 with dots; contraction orange #E69F00 with horizontal lines; churn vermillion #D55E00 with cross-hatch. Pattern fills make every category distinguishable without color; every bar carries a text label and value.
- A "base" bracket used to show which bars NRR includes.
