## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: "more data on the slide is more convincing." The animation morphs the analyst's twelve-column, six-segment stacked chart (plus pie) from Lesson 4 into the two-line assertion chart, one step at a time, so learners see each design decision as a deletion that serves the headline. It ends by cutting to the tenure-vs-parts four-bar comparison.

## Visual language

- Start state uses six muted category fills plus a legend, deliberately busy.
- End state: grey #7F7F7F thin line for first-visit calls; accent orange #E69F00 heavy line for callbacks, also marked with circular point markers so it differs by shape, not just color. Direct end-of-line labels.
- Each step is named by an on-screen chip ("1. Write the sentence").

## Scenes

| # | Duration | Frame description | Motion | Caption / VO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8s | Analyst's chart: 12 stacked columns, 6 segments, legend in tiny type, plus a pie. Title "Service Call Volume by Reason, FY24." | A small timer reads 2 s, then "Point understood? No." | "Here's the report Bay Ridge sent. Two seconds — what's the point? There isn't one. It's a report." |
| 2 | 10s | Chip: "1. Write the sentence." Headline types out: "Callback visits grew 31% while first-visit demand stayed flat." | Typewriter. | "Start with the claim, in their language. Everything that doesn't serve this sentence goes." |
| 3 | 8s | The pie slides off-screen labeled "repeats the columns." | Slide out. | "The pie repeats the columns. Gone." |
| 4 | 12s | Chip: "2. Collapse to what the claim needs." Five non-callback segments merge into one grey block per month labeled "First-visit calls"; callback segment stays separate. | Segments merge. | "Six categories become the two the sentence is about." |
| 5 | 12s | Chip: "3. Match the form to the claim." Columns morph into two lines across 12 months: grey flat ~2,180; orange rising 440 → 575. | Bars collapse into points; lines draw. | "It's a claim about change over time with two series. That's a line chart." |
| 6 | 10s | Chip: "4. Label directly; delete the legend." Legend fades; labels appear at line ends. "+31%" callout attaches to the orange line end. | Fade/attach. | "Labels where the eye already is. One callout on the mark itself." |
| 7 | 8s | Chip: "5. Source it." Source line appears bottom-left: "Bay Ridge dispatch export, Jan–Dec, received 14 May." Gridlines thin to one. | Fade. | "A source line on customer data, every time. The nineteen-year supervisor will ask." |
| 8 | 12s | Hard cut to the turn slide: two panels, four bars. Tenure: 21% vs 18%. Parts on truck: 4% vs 71%. Headline: "Tenure barely separates your callbacks. Parts availability separates them almost completely." | Bars grow; 71% bar in accent with label. | "And the slide the whole presentation turns on — four bars. The argument is visible without a word of narration." |

## Interaction variant

Step-through version where the learner applies each of the five steps to the Fairmount "48-bar grouped chart" from Lesson 4 Exercise 4, choosing from options at each step and seeing the chart update; wrong choices (e.g., truncated bar axis at 95.5) show a warning explaining why.

## Production notes

- Values must match Lesson 4 (2,180 flat; 440 → 575; 21/18; 4/71).
- Keep the start state legible enough to be captioned honestly; its busyness is the point, not unreadable noise.
- Provide a static PNG sequence of the five steps for slide-based courses.
