## Summary
CHW105 is practical and well sequenced: settings research, then portfolio and LinkedIn, then a mentor-and-packet project with a clear definition of done. The highest-value improvement is hands-on de-identification practice, because the portfolio rule is the one most likely to cause real harm if learners get it wrong; project x01 supplies a planted-identifier lab. Several statements about Medi-Cal and CHW credentials are time-sensitive and should be checked each cohort.

## Depth and coverage gaps
- **De-identification is taught as a checklist but never practiced on a planted example.** Project x01 adds one. Objective: "Build a LinkedIn profile and professional portfolio that evidence CHW skills."
- **No worked learning agreement.** The packet requires three to five behavioral goals mapped to competencies; one full worked example (with competency IDs from the pathway framework) would reduce resubmissions. Objective: "Complete required planning documentation accurately and on time."
- **No sample informational-interview email or mentor follow-up email.** Add one model of each. Objective: "Select a field mentor and submit complete internship planning documentation."
- **Workforce research table** would benefit from a filled example row for each setting using fictional organizations. Objective: "Research California CHW workforce settings and identify a fit for internship placement."
- **Check-your-understanding** added to Lesson 2.

## Open questions for the course owner
- **Medi-Cal CHW benefit:** verify current DHCS requirements (who may recommend services, supervising provider rules, qualification pathways such as certificate or work-experience routes, any annual training requirement) before each cohort; the lesson is appropriately general.
- **California CHW certification:** state-level CHW certificate standards have been under development; confirm what, if any, state certification exists now and how the lesson and CHW106's resume guidance ("state certification pending") should describe it.
- **Program forms:** the packet references "your program's forms"; should the course link the actual forms or a template?
- **Compliance clearances:** TB, immunization, and background check requirements vary by site; confirm the program's standard list.
