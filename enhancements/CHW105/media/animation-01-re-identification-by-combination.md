## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: "If I remove the name, it's de-identified." The animation starts with a crowd of 50,000 dots (a county) and filters it down detail by detail — a town, an age range, a language, a condition, a building — until one dot remains, even though no name was ever used. Then it shows the generalized version that leaves a crowd.

## Visual language

- Dots representing people on a dark-blue background; remaining dots in white, filtered dots fade to gray.
- Each filter appears as a labeled text chip ("Town of ~600", "Age 91", "Speaks Tongan", "Former town midwife", "Rosewood Apartments").
- A counter in large numerals shows how many people still match.
- No color is required to read the state: the counter and chip text carry the meaning.

## Interaction variant

A clickable version: learners toggle each detail on and off and watch the counter. Goal: keep the sentence useful as evidence of skill while keeping the counter in the thousands.
