## Purpose

After watching, the learner can describe a typical day in each of the five CHW settings, score a placement on four fit criteria, and name the logistics that most often derail a placement late.

## Audience and prerequisites

CHW105 learners at the start of Lesson 2. Presenters: five short "day in the life" segments by fictional composite CHWs (actors), framed by an instructor host.

## Script

| Time | Visual / On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Host on camera. | HOST: "Search 'community health worker' in any California county and you'll get one title and five very different jobs. Let's meet five composite CHWs — they're actors, and their days are drawn from the lesson — and then talk about how you pick." |

| 1:50 | Lower third: "County public health — 'Andre' (composite)." Health fair B-roll. | ANDRE: "I work on immunization outreach and a home-visiting program for new parents. Hiring took months — civil service — but the job's stable. I spend a lot of time on events, materials, and data." |
| 2:20 | Lower third: "Hospital / health system — 'Kim' (composite)." ED corridor B-roll (no patients visible). | KIM: "Emergency department navigation. I might meet someone once, at a bad moment, and my job is that they leave with a plan and a real connection. It's fast, and shifts can be scheduled." |
| 2:50 | Host. Graphic: four-criterion scorecard — Population and language • Skills you'll build • Supervision • Logistics and capacity. | HOST: "Now pick. Score each site on four things. Population and language: can you talk directly with the people you'll serve? Skills: will you assess, educate, coordinate, document — or file and staff a table? Supervision: is there a named person, with time, who's supervised a student before? And logistics: commute, schedule, background check, TB clearance, immunizations." |
| 3:40 | Host, closer. Text: "Supervision is the one people underweight." | HOST: "Supervision is the criterion people underweight. Good supervision turns an average site into a great placement. Its absence sinks a great site." |

## Check for understanding

1. Which setting most often generates member lists from data and tracks outreach attempts? *Answer: Medi-Cal health plans.*
2. Name the four fit criteria. *Answer: population and language, skills you'll build, supervision, logistics and capacity.*
3. What is the most common reason a planning packet is late, and how do you prevent it? *Answer: onboarding clearances such as background checks and TB tests; start them as soon as the site is confirmed and work backward from the deadline.*
