## Purpose

After watching, the learner can spot a CTR outlier in Search Console, rule out the alternatives, rewrite the title and meta description to lesson 06's standard, and state the expected impact as a range.

## Audience and prerequisites

Learners who have completed lesson 06's title and meta description sections and lesson 11's section on the Performance report.

## Check for understanding

1. Why compare this page's CTR with other Meridian pages at position 6 to 8 rather than a published CTR curve? **Answer:** published curves come from different data sets, query mixes, and years; your own site's pages at the same position on similar SERPs are the honest benchmark.
2. Name two alternatives you must rule out before blaming the title. **Answer:** any two of: new SERP furniture above the result, a lost rich result in Search appearance, a device-specific truncation, an average position hiding very different positions per query.
3. Why claim only half the gap? **Answer:** the benchmark is an average across different pages and queries; assuming a full close overstates the likely gain and sets an expectation you can't defend.
