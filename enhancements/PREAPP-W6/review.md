## Summary

Week 6 has excellent worked examples: the P1-P7 analytics table and the campaign card are the best "show, don't tell" material in the program, and the arithmetic checks out. ## Depth and coverage gaps

- **No worked deconstruction** for "Identify which element of a post drives attention, retention, and response." The lesson describes the method; a fully annotated example post (hook, fold, shape, CTA, and how each explains its engagement) would model Practice 2.
- **Campaign execution is out of scope** of the lessons (the calendar is executed in Weeks 7-8), but no artifact captures whether it worked. The supplementary project adds a tracker, mid-flight memo, and results card (objective: "Design a simple campaign with audience, message, channel, cadence, and metric").
- **No end-of-rotation reflection assigned**; added in the project.
- **Positioning statement examples are all developer-flavored.** Add one each for sales, marketing, and AI pathways so learners leaning toward those pathways see themselves (objective: "Craft a positioning statement and deconstruct high-performing content into hook, structure, and CTA").
