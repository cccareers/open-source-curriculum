## Summary
CHW106 is a well-designed capstone: requirements are numbered and confirmable, confidentiality is a hard gate, and the evidence log ties practice to the resume. The main opportunities are a worked practice log (learners currently mine only their own, often thin, logs), a modeled log-to-bullet transformation, and careful wording about California CHW credentials, which are in flux.

## Depth and coverage gaps
- **No practice log to learn on.** Project x01 supplies a fictional 12-week log. Objective: "Translate internship experience into quantified, verifiable statements."
- **Supervision agenda template missing.** R9 requires a written agenda for each supervision contact; a one-page template (did / unsure / need decided / decisions recorded) would raise quality. Objective: "Bring boundary and safety concerns to a field mentor early."
- **Mandated reporting pathway (R8)** is correctly generic; a California-specific worksheet reviewed by a qualified person would help interns fill it in. Objective: "Work with real clients while maintaining confidentiality, boundaries, and supervision practices."
- **Interview preparation** is not covered beyond documents; a short section or project on turning log entries into interview answers would complete the launch track (maps to "Refine a resume, cover letter, and professional bio for CHW roles" without adding a new objective — treat as stretch practice only).
- **Check-your-understanding** added to Lesson 5.
