## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: "Boundary violations are dramatic choices that bad workers make." Reality: drift is gradual, each step feels like kindness, and the warning sign is a decision you would rather not mention in supervision. The animation shows a timeline of small steps sliding down a gentle slope, with a "mention it in supervision?" gate at each step, and contrasts the early-raised path with the silent path.

## Visual language

- A gentle downhill path drawn left to right labeled "Week 1" to "Week 8."
- An intern figure walks the path. Each step is a labeled sign (text).
- At each step, a small gate labeled "Raise in supervision?" with "Yes" (path flattens) or "Not yet" (slope steepens).
- Colors: blue #0072B2 for the supervised path, vermilion #D55E00 for the silent path; both paths also labeled in text ("Raised early," "Kept quiet").

## Interaction variant

A choose-your-path H5P: at each gate the learner picks "Raise it now" or "Handle it myself." The slope angle and a "trust meter" respond; at the end, learners see the supervision agenda line they would have written for each step.
