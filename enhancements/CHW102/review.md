## Summary
CHW102 is a well-built orientation to the ten C3 roles, with rich practice sets and a strong closing story (Ms. Reyes) that shows every role in sequence. The most important fixes are three cross-references that point to CHW105 for content that actually lives in CHW103 (confidentiality, boundaries, documentation). The biggest opportunity is to turn the Ms. Reyes story into a worked case file learners can practice on (project x01), and to give the interpreter etiquette section a modeled demonstration.

## Depth and coverage gaps
- **Interpreter etiquette is described, not shown.** A modeled before/after encounter would help (drafted as video v01). Objective: "Use language access resources appropriately in mediation situations."
- **CLAS is explained at theme level only.** That is appropriate for the objective, but learners would benefit from a one-page crosswalk of the 15 standards to the three themes as a handout. Objective: "Explain cultural mediation and humility, including the CLAS standards."
- **Screening section** would benefit from one fully worked example of a positive screen moving through protocol (consent → administer → immediate-danger rule → record → route), using a fictional client. Objective: "Describe the CHW role in screenings and multi-provider care coordination."
- **No answer keys** for the many role-identification items; ambiguous items (e.g., the herbal tea example in Lesson 2) should have an instructor key with rationale. Objective: "Describe the C3 framework roles that define the scope of CHW practice."
- **Check-your-understanding** added to Lesson 3 in this pass; Lessons 2, 4, 5, and 6 would benefit from the same.

## Assessment ideas
- Role-identification bank (30 items) with keyed primary/secondary roles and scope flags.
- Scenario quiz on child interpretation: learners pick the correct action and documentation.
- Referral-loop audit: given a fictional referral log, mark each referral's state and the evidence missing.
- Rubric for the adaptation redesign (Lesson 2 Practice 7): four dials changed, clinical content unchanged and stated.
