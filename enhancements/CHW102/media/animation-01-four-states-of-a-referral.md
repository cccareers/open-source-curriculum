## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: "I made the referral, so my part is done." The animation shows 100 referrals entering a pipe with four stages and leaking at each joint for realistic reasons (wrong number, eligibility, waitlist, fear, transportation). Then it shows CHW follow-up patching each leak, and ends by contrasting the output ("referrals made") with the outcome ("needs resolved").

## Visual language

- A horizontal pipe in four segments labeled MADE → SCHEDULED → ATTENDED → RESOLVED.
- Referrals are small dots (blue #0072B2). Leaking dots fall out of joints into a tray labeled with the reason in text.
- Patches are orange (#E69F00) bands with a wrench icon and a label (e.g., "Follow-up call day 3").
- Counters above each segment show numbers in text. Okabe-Ito palette; reasons are always written, never color-only.

## Interaction variant

A scrubbable version where the learner drags a slider to add each CHW practice and watches the "Resolved" counter respond. Alternative: H5P drag-and-drop where learners match each leak reason to the CHW action that patches it.

## Production notes

- All numbers are illustrative and must be labeled as such on screen; they are not research findings.
- Reasons in the trays match Lesson 4's troubleshooting list, so learners recognize them.
- 80 seconds at 30 fps; keep sound cues subtle and provide a no-audio version with captions.
