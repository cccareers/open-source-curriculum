## Concept and misconception it fixes

Two misconceptions: (1) "After Demo Day I can rest a week, then follow up," and (2) "My contacts are safe because they're in the CRM and on LinkedIn." The animation shows contact warmth decaying over days without a touch, and each dated follow-up resetting it. It then switches off program accounts and shows what remains in a personal, verified export.

## Visual language (shapes, colors with color-blind-safe palette, labels)

- Contacts are cards with a "warmth" bar (a thermometer icon with a numeric label 0-100). Warmth falls over time.
- A calendar strip along the bottom shows Day 0 (Demo Day) to Day 30.
- A follow-up is an envelope icon that refills the warmth bar.
- Palette (Okabe-Ito): warmth bar in orange #E69F00 (high) fading to sky blue #56B4E9 (low), always with a number on the bar; program-owned systems shown with a building icon and a gray badge "PROGRAM"; personal storage shown with a house icon and a bluish green #009E73 badge "YOURS."

## Interaction variant (optional)

A 30-day scrubber: learners schedule follow-ups for five contacts by dragging envelopes onto days and watch the warmth bars respond. A second tab is a checklist simulator where an export missing the notes column fails verification.

## Production notes

- The warmth bar is a **metaphor**, not a measured decay rate. Do not show percentages implying research-based numbers; label the axis "how well they remember you (illustrative)."
- Match the lesson's timing language: thank-you within 48 hours; days 1-3, 4-10, 11-20, 21-30 tracks.
- Keep platform names generic (CRM, LinkedIn) consistent with the lesson's vendor-neutral stance on CRM.
