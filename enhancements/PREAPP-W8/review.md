## Depth and coverage gaps

- **No model talk** for "Deliver a problem, approach, evidence, and ask narrative to a mixed audience." A one-page sample spine (problem, approach, evidence, ask paragraphs) would help learners who freeze on a blank page.
- **No guidance on answering "where does that number come from?"** live, though W8-03 requires that every number be sourceable within a minute. The supplementary project adds a source card and drill.
- **Transition plan has no worked example** for "Complete a 30-day transition plan and verify ownership of the network and data built." The default table is good; one filled-in plan with real dates and calibrated targets would model Practice 1-2.
- **Data ownership omits the evidence trail itself.** Learners should archive their claim register and evidence (or equivalent) along with CRM and LinkedIn, because the portfolio's credibility depends on them later.

## Assessment ideas

- **Feedback quality check:** ten peer notes; learners mark which have where/what/what-instead and rewrite two.
- **Spine identification:** a 2-minute transcript; learners label problem, approach, evidence, ask, and note what is missing.
- **Milestone testability:** five draft milestones; learners rewrite untestable ones with a number, date, and source of truth.
