## Scenario

At Demo Day an employer partner says to you afterward: "That reply-rate jump was interesting — where does that number come from?" You have about thirty seconds before the next person cuts in. This project gets you ready for that moment and the thirty days after it. You build a small **evidence pack** (the slides and leave-behind that carry your numbers, each traced to the claim register you built in Week 7), run a **source-on-demand drill**, and hand everything off into a thirty-day plan and a personal archive you own.

It closes the eight-week chain: the ledger you opened in Week 1 becomes the evidence on stage and the archive you keep.

## What you will produce

1. **Evidence pack**: one or two evidence slides and a one-page leave-behind (name, headline result with denominator, portfolio URL, QR code, your ask).
2. **Source card**: an index card or phone note listing each number you will say on stage, its source, and a one-sentence explanation.
3. **Hallway kit**: one follow-up sentence per audience group (staff, employer partners, network contacts) and a contact-capture method that lands in your CRM the same day.
4. **Hand-off bundle**: your thirty-day plan seeded with every Demo Day contact, plus a verified personal archive that includes the evidence ledger, claim register, portfolio source, and CRM export.

## Before you start (prerequisites, starter files or data)

- Presentation spine and deck (Lesson 2, Practice 1-3).
- Claim register from the Week 7 project, or your W7-04 verification notes.
- Week 6 campaign tracker and named list (for knowing who is coming).
- Evidence ledger, Weeks 1-7.

## Milestones

1. **Choose the stage numbers from the register** (30 min). Pick one headline number and at most two supporting numbers, each already verified in the Week 7 claim register. Write each with its denominator ("19% of 214 contacts").
2. **Build the evidence slides** (45 min). One number per slide, large enough for the back row (Lesson 2 rules). A footnote line on each slide names the source in plain language ("CRM export, Weeks 1-7, first touches only").
3. **Write the source card** (20 min). For each number: where it lives, how it was counted, and a one-sentence answer to "Where does that come from?" that you can say in under fifteen seconds.
4. **Source-on-demand drill** (30 min, in pairs). Your partner plays an employer partner and asks about two numbers at random. You answer from the card, then open the actual source on your phone or laptop. Target: under one minute each. Repeat until clean.
5. **Leave-behind and hallway kit** (40 min). One page: name, pathway, headline result with denominator, portfolio URL, QR code (test it with two different phones), and your ask. Write your three follow-up sentences. Set up the fastest way to capture a contact (a CRM mobile form, a sign-up sheet, or a note template) and practice logging one contact in under a minute.
6. **Demo Day** (the event). Present. Capture every person who approaches you and log each in the CRM with a dated next action before you leave (Lesson 3 definition of done).
7. **Seed the thirty-day plan** (40 min, within 48 hours). Every Demo Day contact gets a follow-up row in days 1-3 of the plan and a CRM task. Add your Week 6 campaign results card (prediction vs. actual attendance).
8. **Archive and verify** (45 min). Add the final Week 8 numbers row, a caption card for the evidence pack, and a dated hard/boring/surprising reflection. Export the CRM (Lesson 4). Copy the evidence ledger, claim register, portfolio source, playbook, and Week 5 repository link into one dated folder in a personal account, with a second copy elsewhere. Run Lesson 4's verification checklist, and also open the ledger from the archive copy to confirm it is complete.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every number on a slide or the leave-behind has a denominator and appears in the verified claim register.
- [ ] Partner drill: two random numbers each sourced in under a minute.
- [ ] The QR code and portfolio URL both work on two different phones and in a private browser window.
- [ ] Every Demo Day contact is in the CRM with a dated next action, logged the same day.
- [ ] The thirty-day plan includes a follow-up for every Demo Day contact within 48 hours.
- [ ] The archive folder is in a personal account, dated, duplicated, and includes the ledger and CRM export; ten random rows checked.

## Evidence checklist

- Evidence slides and leave-behind (PDF)
- Source card (photo or text)
- Partner drill log with times
- CRM screenshot of Demo Day contacts with next actions
- Thirty-day plan
- Archive folder listing with dates (screenshot)

## Rubric

| Criterion | Developing | Meets | Exceeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence on stage | Numbers without denominators or sources | Each number has a denominator and a plain-language source line | Numbers chosen to support the ask aimed at employer partners |
| Answering "where's that from?" | Vague or slow answer | Answer in under fifteen seconds, source opened in under a minute | Also states a limitation of the number unprompted |
| Lead capture | Contacts on paper or unlogged | All logged same day with dated next actions | Each follow-up references something specific the person said |
| Ownership | Archive incomplete or in a program account | Personal, dated, duplicated, verified | Re-export reminder set and a recovery address confirmed |
| Plan realism | Targets copied from the lesson | Targets calibrated to own program averages | Weekly review already scheduled and first review completed |

## Stretch goals

- Record a three-minute version of your talk for contacts who missed Demo Day, and add it to the portfolio.
- Send your Week 6 results card as part of a short "thank you for coming" note to everyone on your named list who attended.

## Reflection prompts

- Open your Week 1 ledger row beside your final row. Which number moved most, and what was the change behind it?
- Which question from the audience could you not answer? What would you need to find out?

## Instructor notes (common pitfalls, how to adapt for time)

- **Pitfall: new numbers on Demo Day.** "No new content" (Lesson 3) also applies to numbers. If a learner recomputes on the morning of the event, it must go through the claim register first.
- **Pitfall: unlogged leads.** Hold the lesson's rule: logged before leaving the building.
- **Program accounts:** if the cohort CRM is program-provisioned, confirm export permissions well before the last day. Learners cannot verify ownership after access ends.
- **Short on time:** Milestones 1, 3, 4, 6, 8 are the core (about 2.5 hours plus the event).
- **Arc:** final link (`enhancements/PREAPP-ARC.md`). The archive from milestone 8 is the participant's permanent copy of eight weeks of evidence.
