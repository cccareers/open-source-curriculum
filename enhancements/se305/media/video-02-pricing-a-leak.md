## Purpose

After watching, the learner can price a below-reference conversion step in annual won ACV by carrying the extra opportunities through their own downstream rates, rank leaks by dollars rather than percentage points, and say why that ranking drives the plan.

## Audience and prerequisites

Learners who have read Lesson 4 through "Step 3: price the leak." Figures come from Lessons 3–4: a 96-opportunity closed cohort; step rates of 60.4% / 62.1% / 83.3% / 80.0%; team medians of 74% / 71% / 78% / 76%; average won deal $28,500.

## On-screen assets and B-roll

- Whiteboard funnel pipes with dots flowing; ranking table; formula box.

## Accessibility

- Captions; every calculation is spoken as well as written.
- Rates above and below the median are marked with up and down arrows and text, not color alone.
- A worked arithmetic sheet is provided.

## Check for understanding

1. Why do you carry the extra opportunities through your own downstream rates rather than the team's? *(Answer: you're isolating the value of fixing one step. Everything else has to stay as it actually is for you.)*
2. In the class data, which leak is worth more, and by how much? *(Answer: discovery to validation, $153,330 against $98,610, about $54,700 more.)*
3. What has to accompany every rate you quote in a diagnosis? *(Answer: its sample size.)*
