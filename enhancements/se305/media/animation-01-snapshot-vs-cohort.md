## Concept and misconception it fixes

Misconception: dividing one stage's count by the previous stage's count in a snapshot gives conversion. The animation shows deals as dots entering and leaving stages over time; freezing at any instant produces a snapshot whose ratios depend on how fast each stage empties. It then replays only the 96 closed deals as a cohort, producing the Lesson 3 step conversions (60.4%, 62.1%, 83.3%, 80.0%) and the "died in stage" counts.

## Visual language

- Four vertical stage lanes left to right: Discovery, Solution validation, Proposal, Negotiation; exits at the right labeled "Won" and at the bottom of each lane labeled "Lost."
- Dots: open deals hollow circles (grey #999999); won deals solid bluish green #009E73 with a check glyph; lost deals vermillion #D55E00 with an × glyph. Shape and glyph carry meaning, not color alone.
- A camera-shutter icon for "snapshot."
