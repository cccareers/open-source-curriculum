## Scenario

You have just interviewed for an SDR role, and the sales manager says: "Send me something that shows how you think about a pipeline. One page, and a short clip of you on a call if you have one." This project produces exactly that package from the work you already did this week — the recorded mock discovery call and your pipeline review — tightened into something a stranger can evaluate in five minutes.

It is also your **Technical Sales rotation artifact** for the Week 7 Outreach Portfolio, which requires at least one artifact from every rotation.

## What you will produce

1. **A call review sheet** for your recorded mock discovery call: timestamped clips, scores, and three self-critiques.
2. **A one-page funnel brief**: funnel table with conversion rates, weakest stage, evidence, corrective plan, what you are stopping.
3. **Ledger updates**: Week 3 numbers row, two caption cards (call review, funnel brief), dated Week 3 reflection.

## Before you start (prerequisites, starter files or data)

- Your recorded mock discovery call (Lesson 3, Practice 3), with the practitioner's permission to keep the recording for your own review. Do **not** publish the recording without the practitioner's explicit permission.
- Pipeline scored 0-2 on need, authority, timing (Lesson 3, Practice 4).
- Verbatim objection log and funnel spreadsheet (Lesson 4, Practice 2-3).
- Your evidence ledger (Weeks 1-2).

Call review template:

```text
CALL REVIEW — mock discovery, [date], practitioner-as-buyer: [role only]
Talk time (mine): __%       Follow-up questions asked: __
Phase timings: Open __ | Context __ | Need __ | Authority+Timing __ | Confirm-back __ | Next step __

Need surfaced (their words):        "..."   at mm:ss
Authority surfaced:                 "..."   at mm:ss
Timing surfaced:                    "..."   at mm:ss
Best follow-up question:            "..."   at mm:ss
Moment I started pitching:          mm:ss — what I should have asked instead: "..."
What I failed to surface:           [need / authority / timing] — the question that would have found it: "..."
Outcome: [next step + date] or [explicit no-fit + reason]
```

## Milestones

1. **Rewatch and time-stamp** (60 min). Watch your recording once with the template open. Fill every field; use timestamps, not impressions. Estimate talk time by timing your own speaking turns in the first five minutes and the last five minutes and averaging them.
2. **Write three rewrites** (20 min). For the three weakest questions you asked, write the open-question version using the Lesson 3 table as a model.
3. **Finalize the funnel table** (30 min). Use "reached at least this stage" counts (Lesson 4, "Your pipeline is a funnel"). Include the sixth row for qualification decisions (advance / nurture / disqualify counts).
4. **Write the brief** (45 min). One page, five parts in order: numbers, weakest stage, evidence (cite the objection tally and message versions), corrective plan with a date and a measurable target, what you are stopping. Every number traces to your CRM or spreadsheet.
5. **Pressure-test the plan** (20 min). A partner asks: "Does your fix match the cause?" If your weakest stage is messaged → replied and your fix is "send more," rewrite it (Lesson 4: scaling a failure).
6. **Update the ledger** (25 min). Week 3 row with new columns `qualified_advance`, `qualified_nurture`, `disqualified`, `weakest_stage`. Two caption cards. Dated reflection: hard, boring, surprising — and one sentence on whether a full-time SDR seat appeals to you, with your reason.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Call review has every field filled with timestamps and quoted language.
- [ ] Three weak questions rewritten as open, neutral questions that name no product or service.
- [ ] Funnel table uses "reached at least" counts and correct stage-to-stage arithmetic.
- [ ] Brief fits on one page and has all five parts in order.
- [ ] Corrective plan names one change, a target number, and a date, and addresses the stated cause.
- [ ] At least one item honestly reports something that went badly.
- [ ] Ledger has the Week 3 row, two caption cards, and a dated reflection.

## Evidence checklist

- Call review sheet (document)

## Rubric

| Criterion | Developing | Meets | Exceeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Call self-assessment | General impressions ("went OK") | Timestamped evidence for each of need, authority, timing | Identifies a pattern across the call (e.g. pitching after every closed question) and a habit to fix it |
| Question craft | Rewrites still closed or leading | Three rewrites are open, neutral, product-free | Each rewrite includes the follow-up you would ask after the likely answer |
| Funnel accuracy | Current-stage counts or arithmetic errors | Correct cumulative counts and rates | Notes whether the weakest-rate stage is also the biggest absolute loss, and why it matters |
| Corrective plan | "Do better at X" | One change, measurable, dated, matched to cause | Includes the result you expect and how you will know by the date |
| Honesty | Only flattering numbers | Weak stage stated plainly, early | Also names what the data cannot tell you yet |

## Reflection prompts

- Which objection did you hear most, and what does it say about your targeting or message rather than about the people?
- If you were paid on meetings booked, which of your Week 2 behaviors would change? Would that be better or worse?

## Instructor notes (common pitfalls, how to adapt for time)

- **Recording consent:** confirm the practitioner agreed to the recording being kept by the learner. The portfolio should cite the call review sheet, not embed the video, unless permission is explicit.
- **Pitfall: current-stage counts** produce falling conversion rates; check the table before the brief is written.
- **Pitfall: fix doesn't match cause.** The most common mismatch is a targeting or message problem answered with volume.
- **Short on time:** Milestones 1, 3, 4 (about 2.25 hours). The brief can double as the speaker notes for the live pipeline review.
- **Arc:** third link (`enhancements/PREAPP-ARC.md`). The funnel table format here becomes the input format for the Week 5 funnel script.
