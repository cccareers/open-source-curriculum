## Summary

These lessons explain sales roles, discovery, and funnel reading clearly and with practitioner credibility. The biggest gap is translation. The lessons teach the framework in a vendor-selling frame (buyer, budget, product), but the practice items have learners score their *job-search* pipeline, and nothing tells them what "need, authority, timing" means for a hiring contact. The second gap is a funnel-counting ambiguity carried over from Week 1.

## Depth and coverage gaps

- **No full example call** for "Apply a discovery framework in a live mock call and score the pipeline against qualification criteria." The lesson gives the six-part shape and a question table but no transcript. The drafted video gives two contrasting calls with scoring.
- **Objection handling covers three objections**, all from a vendor-call context. Learners in a job-search pipeline mostly hear "we're not hiring right now," "apply through the portal," and "I'm not the right person." A follow-up pass could add these three with acknowledge/explore/reframe responses (objective: "Handle objections and analyze the personal pipeline as a funnel").
- **Metric table has no worked behavior example** for "Relate metrics such as meetings booked and pipeline created to the role that owns them." A two-rep comparison (same activity, different meetings-held quality) would make "metrics shape behavior" concrete.
- **No end-of-rotation reflection is assigned**, yet W7-02 relies on "a reflection at the end of every rotation." The supplementary project adds a dated reflection with a "would I want this seat" question.

## Assessment ideas

- **Question classification:** 15 questions; learners tag open/closed/leading and rewrite three.
- **Funnel calculation:** given current-stage CRM counts, compute "reached at least" counts, conversion rates, and the weakest stage.
- **Fix-to-cause matching:** five funnel scenarios, five candidate fixes; learners match and justify.
